# Product intent, reference research and visible design decisions

Product UI Experience Alignment connects current users, tasks and operating conditions to a concrete target experience. UI Spec Studio finds useful mature references whether the user supplies a product name, a functional goal or an existing product to improve. The [alignment and exploration guide](PRODUCT-ALIGNMENT.md) explains how research questions become observed design relationships and verifiable implementation choices.

Begin with a revisable experience hypothesis. Distinguish the current user's
intent, relevant user-group experience and the reference product's actual
behavior. Preserve version, platform, role and observation conditions where they
affect fit. Plan text, visual and behavioral evidence around tasks, parts, states
and their relationships. The [experience research guide](EXPERIENCE-RESEARCH.md)
explains this planning and its connection to continued exploration.

Record task requirements and explicit aesthetic goals together. Familiarity can
come from understandable objects, labels, outcomes and continuation while layout,
type, material and motion develop an independent expression. A professional
product's category supplies context; the requested artistic direction remains a
delivery requirement. See the [art-direction guide](ART-DIRECTION.md).

## Mature work is the default design starting point

Design theory and UI resource knowledge work together throughout the process:
product understanding → paired reference/resource exploration → candidate
comparison → local trial → adoption and adaptation → whole-experience verification.
New discoveries can refine the initial concept. References establish appropriate
craft targets; adopted implementations carry them into the product. Compare
readability, hierarchy, detail and state continuity in the actual task; identity
and surface form remain product choices.

For a new surface, explore the highest appropriate reuse level: a complete page or
design system, a functional block, then the required components and effects.
Inherit coherent hierarchy, proportions, typography rhythm and interaction
relationships through suitable existing implementations. Configure, compose and
adapt them while preserving their defining craft. A demonstrated gap requires further sourcing or a qualifying, explicitly authorized W1–W3 original scope. Data connections and
adaptation code can carry reuse; a studied relationship alone establishes design
inheritance. For a local correction, an accepted and still-suitable product can
already be the foundation.

## Recognize use before fixing the shell

A domain name such as research platform identifies subject matter. The first
meaningful action, enduring object, action/result loop, continuation and outcome
location identify the interaction model. Conversational assistants, document
spaces, task systems and editors organize those relationships differently. A
hybrid states which loop is primary and when its secondary surface becomes useful.

The common analysis reference contains this reasoning. It also distinguishes
observed behavior, inferred direction and an unresolved product preference.
Screenshot feedback supports visible observations; code and live tasks establish
what the actual product can do.

## Study complementary reference roles

**Functional and experience references** explain task, object, state and continuity relationships. **Artistic and expression references** expand the available subject, composition, type, material and motion language, including useful cross-domain work. **Implementation resources** supply adoptable templates, systems, blocks, components, assets or interaction code. The first two are design-reference roles; a source can also provide implementation. Record which role each observation supports. Transferring a technique creates a design basis and leaves the authored implementation responsible for its own quality.

Research these roles together while the model, layout and treatment can still change. Expand a named product into the relevant surface and task; for a functional brief, find candidates from the primary loop and uncertain relationships. Follow visual clues through playback, scrolling and state changes. Attribute the evidence to the hosting page, player, demonstrated UI or its content, and distinguish observed behavior from inferred cause.

Describe what is useful, which conditions it relies on and what the current product will inherit or adjust. Before wider integration, trial consequential candidates with current fonts, content, theme and states. Distinguish the mature reference, original resource demo, local trial and assembled journey. Suitable family members can share already established evidence under the same conditions. Revisit the original quality question after adaptation and in continuous use.

Apply [multiscale perceptual inspection](VISUAL-INSPECTION.md) while making that
choice: establish the whole-page impression at normal scale, examine composition,
regions and high-impact details, then return to the surrounding interface. State
the visible relationship rather than relying on an adjective alone. Optical
weight, typography, boundary contrast and material can explain craft at different
expression levels within the same product; the task and explicit direction
determine suitable emphasis.

Start adaptation with content, public configuration and composition. Identify the purpose and affected states of internal changes; substantial changes to layout, state or motion require corresponding inspection of the current implementation. Preserve useful source evidence while judging the version actually delivered.

## Inherit at the right layer

Existing functions, interfaces, permissions, data and necessary behavior provide
continuity. Existing visual components are candidates assessed against the new
target. Explicit accepted design has its own scope and provenance. A local UI
can be retained for demonstrated fit; another may be adapted or replaced while
its reliable functional contracts stay intact.

## Make the right choice together

When credible alternatives materially change the primary action, information
architecture or whole-product identity, offer a recommendation with a small
number of distinct options. Use comparable content, concrete sources and a
bounded preview where useful. Ask for the user's intended experience, not a
catalog of component decisions.

For an artistic direction, make alternatives visibly distinct through subject,
composition or motion language, with comparable care and relevant populated
states. Identify the task relationships retained and the visual expectations
reorganized. Color substitutions alone cover a narrower theme choice.

Clear intent, accepted direction or delegated autonomy supports direct progress.
One-shot work can deliver a well-supported provisional recommendation and identify
its open choices. User approval is recorded from its actual source, not inferred
from a working preview or successful test.

## Pair the selection record with the preview

The record connects each consequential surface/dimension to its intended experience, research question, correctly attributed observation, meaningful alternatives, selected resource, adaptation, implementation location and observed result. Preserve the relevant source/page/interval and conditions when needed to inspect a decision. Shared references can cover routine members of a component family; depth follows design impact. Include layout/navigation,
background, typography, components, icons, feedback and movement where relevant.
Quiet/native/inherited treatments can be deliberate choices with a brief reason.

Explain which finding strengthened, revised or overturned an earlier assumption,
and what decision follows. A consequential selection should show the different
advantages actually compared and why the chosen direction fits this task. Three
near-identical treatments may support a narrow theme choice while leaving the
larger direction unexplored. Keep documentary facts, observed pixels/behavior,
professional interpretations and unobserved conditions distinguishable.

Use the record to choose the next commitment: continue research, run a local trial
or expand a demonstrated fit. Weigh target understanding, evidence coverage and
depth, candidate diversity, comparison quality, adoption feasibility and remaining
uncertainty. Each factor matters through the decision it could change; an existing
prose record can express it without a fixed form or numerical score.

Keep the visible fact, perceptual interpretation and causal explanation distinct.
An uncertain cause remains a hypothesis until comparison, code or runtime evidence
supports it. Show how the finding changes resource choice or adaptation and what
was seen when the local result returned to the whole page and related states.
Actual user understanding retains its own evidence. Existing records can express
this reasoning in prose; the relationship matters more than a fixed field format.

Keep experience validity and expressive completion findings separate: what works
through use, and whether the promised direction survives in everyday input,
results, navigation and details. For selected or approved source work, record the
fidelity target and intentional changes. Image differences diagnose reproduction;
actual viewing judges the current composition. Link a repair to its specific
constraint and recheck the confirmed expression alongside the task.

Start it as a shortlist, then distinguish researched candidates, completed trials,
actual adoption and verified results. Link important adopted parts to their source,
implementation location, preserved mechanisms and meaningful adaptations. Judge
the responsibilities shaping the main experience; dependency counts and code
ratios describe different properties. Shared rows can serve several surfaces.
Keep the record in existing project documentation; the [template](../skills/ui-spec/assets/templates/design-decisions.md)
is optional. Source, rights and maintenance detail can remain in adoption notes.

Use available evidence first. If consequential material requires normal user
access, request the specific source, operation, states or authorized export that
will resolve the decision. Continue the supported work and retain the difference
between research access and permission to integrate an asset. Keep remaining
quality gaps visible; changes to the target or scope are explicit decisions.

## Inspect a worked scenario

The [research-product study](../examples/product-intent/README.md) demonstrates
conversation-first and document-first options, actual source reuse, a visible
selection panel and working task states. It has its own assumptions, evidence and
pending acceptance. It does not replace the consuming project's product decision.

Run it from source and review the browser evidence. The same method applies to a
service dashboard, editor, public decision flow or screen-based document. See
[the version review](REVIEW-v1.4.0.md) for the change scope and assurance boundaries.

## Content carries design decisions

For consequential user-visible language, the [content decision record](../skills/ui-spec/assets/templates/content-decisions.md) connects user question, role, placement, actual copy, locale variants, source authority and observed behavior. Use existing project records where possible. A revised label or state message can change the layout or journey; render the result together with the selected wording.

## Multiscale evidence and action

Use the same record for an Overview, a selected Design Detail and Execution &
Assurance. Connect L0–L5 objects, transverse states/conditions/dependencies, the
seven decision relations and concrete next work. Challenge the initial inventory
against user tasks and external product evidence. Important traceability is
required; this Markdown template or the optional JSON profile are replaceable
representations. See [the guide](DESIGN-EVIDENCE.md) and the installed
[map](../skills/ui-spec/references/evidence-map.md) and
[action method](../skills/ui-spec/references/evidence-actions.md).
