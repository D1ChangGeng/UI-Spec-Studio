## Execution evidence

Nine actual isolated Agent executions compare the complete v1.17 baseline,
visibility-only guidance and the frozen v1.18 candidate across three task families.
The archive preserves all 46 deliberate original messages, their contemporary
source packages, identical family inputs and final application snapshots. Separate
anonymous product review and process audit inspect the actual results.

The supported finding is earlier and clearer presentation of real results in the
visibility-enhanced conditions. All conditions adopt mature source and make real
corrections. T1 has different useful strengths without an overall ranking; T2
reveals an intermediate keyboard-focus problem in A/C that B avoids in the tested
operation; T3 products are byte-identical. A separate same-SPEC C continuation
corrects that specific failure under independent old/new operation, while retaining
near-edge sampling limits. Its extra feedback and time are not credited to the
original comparison.

The audit also finds a real early-image overwrite. Original limitations remain
visible; an experiment-only recorder now snapshots referenced media independently.
No installed Skill fields or lifecycle changes are introduced by this correction.
