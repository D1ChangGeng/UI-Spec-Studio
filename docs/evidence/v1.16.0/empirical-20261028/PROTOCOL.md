## Outcomes and review

Primary functional outcome: number of the ten published task acceptance groups passed by each output. Preserve group and assertion details, absent product paths and immutable-input violations. Functional assertions are not visual quality scores.

Primary visual outcomes: three independent, fresh-context, version-blinded model reviewers inspect the same matched anonymous packets and use six separate ordinal 0–4 dimensions: coherence; composition/spatial stability; typography/readability; material/asset integration; state/motion communication; brief-specific identity/target alignment. Anchors: 0 missing or too broken to assess; 1 major recurring deficiencies; 2 adequate with conspicuous weaknesses; 3 coherent mature execution with minor weaknesses; 4 exceptionally resolved across the supplied conditions with no material weakness observed. Each dimension requires concrete observed evidence and a limitation. Do not add dimensions into an aesthetic total or treat ordinal differences as percentage improvement. A missing observation is recorded as unassessed, with reason, rather than invented.

T1 identity assesses the complete populated local frontend against the supplied author reference at comparable scope, without pixel-copy or commercial-service parity. T2 identity assesses distinctive expression across populated working views, not a hero alone. T3 identity assesses local sheet craft and continuity with the accepted notebook. Reviewers may inspect prescribed anonymous screenshots and ordered intervals and interact with anonymous local apps; they may not read source, runtime logs, timings, versions or process reports. If continuous video cannot actually be viewed, natural-motion quality is explicitly unassessed. Reviewers lock independent original scores, paired preference, preference strength and reasons before the version map is disclosed. Disagreements remain intact. No reviewer is an independent human user.

Report six matched deltas for each dimension, per-pair three-rater medians and ranges, and paired-preference counts. Do not weight the dimensions or manufacture a universal winner. Three correlated raters do not increase the number of independent generation samples or independent tasks. With only three task families and two repetitions, conclusions are descriptive and conditional; no significance, population-level design uplift, cross-model transfer, production readiness or human usability effect is established.

Secondary behavior outcomes use the frozen process-audit rubric: source discovery and actual observation; evidence changing a consequential choice; mature visual implementation adoption beyond functional primitives; original demo/local trial/integrated inspection continuity; bounded custom changes; T3 scope control. Source and logged actions support findings; unsupported viewing claims are self-reported. Actual retained commands are lower bounds on tools, not total token or cost measurements. Wall time, build attempts and rework are reported without conflating them with model active time or a causal efficiency effect.

Runtime diagnostics include load failures, page/console errors, offline behavior, bundle-resource bytes and relevant geometry/overflow. The shared headless host and parallel activity preclude production frame-rate or device-performance claims.
