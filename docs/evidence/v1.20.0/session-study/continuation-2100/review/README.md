# Independent final product review

The independent reviewer's [final report](reviewer-final-dc68/cohort-01/raw/FINAL.md.txt)
and [original declaration](reviewer-final-dc68/cohort-01/raw/REVIEW-DECLARATION.json)
retain the actual outcome, accepted subsets, exclusions and no-product-repair
judgment. The final declaration is condition-disclosed (`condition_blind=false`)
after both blind cohorts were frozen unchanged.

The [core evidence index](reviewer-final-dc68/indexed-core/review.json) covers the
sixteen original completed products and their actual original image views. The
two prompt-different supplementary replacement products remain separately retained
in [the supplementary evidence locator](reviewer-final-dc68/supplementary-evidence.json).
They are not substituted into the original registered cohort.

The [projection record](reviewer-final-dc68/indexed-core/declaration-projection.json)
explains deterministic ordered-list-to-text conversions and actual verification
path-to-REF mapping required by the existing schema. It retains all original
values and no-repair evidence kinds. Outcome, findings, scope text, limitations
and repair judgment remain reviewer-authored.

The [preservation handoff](reviewer-final-dc68/HANDOFF.md) links the immutable
snapshot, full deduplicated reconstruction, source inventory checks, participant/
view/reference checks, and readback of all three original reviewer freeze manifests.
These engineering proofs establish evidence consistency within their stated scope;
product acceptance is the independent agent judgment retained in the original report.
