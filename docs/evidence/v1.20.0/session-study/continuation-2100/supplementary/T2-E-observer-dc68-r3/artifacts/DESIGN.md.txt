This single formal SPEC keeps composer size, attachment disclosure, response growth, focus and reading position together: they share the viewport budget and runtime state. A component-only result cannot establish continuity.

A long draft should grow within a bounded space while keeping actions accessible. The mature source supplies the visual system; this task authorizes configuration, composition and focused behavior/compatibility repair, with no original visual construction.
