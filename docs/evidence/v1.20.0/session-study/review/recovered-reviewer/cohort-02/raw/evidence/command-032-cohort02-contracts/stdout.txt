## Product goal and audience

Fieldnotes serves a small bilingual research team moving between short observations, long interview synthesis, evidence tables and unfinished arguments. The immediate experience is **read a note, follow a related note, edit a passage, save it, and return without losing the work or the meaning of the material**.

Give this populated workspace a distinctive, mature visual direction based on adoptable external work. Aim for an editorial research studio with a recognisable point of view: strong but useful composition, intentional type relationships, material or colour that carries identity, and clear differences between navigation, reading and editing. The result should feel composed during ordinary work, including the long mixed-language interview, rather than only at an empty start screen. Calm reading is a requirement; visual restraint by itself is not the art direction. Compare materially different mature directions, including one that challenges the initial preference. No final palette, theme, asset or candidate has been selected for the participant.

The complete original task is in `TASK.md`. Preserve its separate responsibilities for experience validity and expressive completion.

## Content and working paths

Eight supplied notes contain synthetic field-research material about a fictional harbour district. They include short English observations, longer Chinese paragraphs, a mixed-language interview, a table, an unfinished draft, and cross-links. Their fictional status is disclosed in the index note. Preserve the notes' meaning, deliberate differences in length, related-note links, tables and unfinished status. Small copy corrections are allowed; shortening content merely to improve a screenshot is not.

The start state opens `Fieldnotes — project index` and `访谈笔记 — 傍晚通勤`. Use the native sidebar/search/tag navigation and the per-note pencil/checkmark controls for the core path. Important tasks:

1. Read the complete interview, inspect `Evidence ledger`, and return to the interview.
2. Edit an English and a Chinese passage, save, and reload to confirm persistence.
3. Start and cancel an edit while preserving the existing note; continue to another note.
4. Find a note by search or tags and reach its editor with the keyboard.
5. Compare long reading and editing in both themes, compact height and narrow width.
