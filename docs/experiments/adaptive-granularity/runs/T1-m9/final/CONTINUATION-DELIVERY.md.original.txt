# Leafnote: clearer reading, with a density choice

The existing notebook now has a labeled **Reading density** preference: **Comfortable** or **Compact**, also available as 舒适 / 紧凑. It applies across the collection and editor and survives reload.

![Comfortable long-note layout](sandbox:/workspace/scratch/43b440780c87/granularity-cells/T1-m9/evidence/continuation/long-1440-en-comfortable-light.png)

## What improved

- **Clearer text roles.** Serif titles have deliberate scaling and wrapping. Body text is 16 px in both modes; metadata and section labels are larger and easier to distinguish.
- **More comfortable long-note reading.** The desktop reading width is bounded. Chinese text gets natural tracking and additional line spacing, independently of the interface language.
- **A useful compact mode.** It reduces title scale, list padding, document spacing and body leading while retaining readable body text and note metadata.
- **A clear control on phones.** Reading density has its own labeled header row, so both options remain understandable and available from the collection and editor.
- **Continuity while changing preferences.** The selected note and unsaved draft survive density, theme and language changes. A failed preference write explains that the setting may not survive the next visit.
