# Leafnote now has a persistent reading-density preference

[Open Leafnote locally](http://localhost:8777/) and use **Reading density** to choose **Comfortable** or **Compact**. The preference applies to the collection and editor together and survives reload.

The typography now gives long notes a clearer hierarchy: prominent titles, a narrower writing measure, more readable supporting text, and consistent body rhythm. On phone-sized screens, project/context labels sit above their field. Very long titles expand completely instead of hiding their final lines inside the old height cap.

![Compact notebook with a long Chinese note](evidence/continuation/run-2/1440-compact-zh-light-editor.png)

## Preserved working behavior

The selected note and unsaved draft survive density, language and theme changes. The selected note is also restored after reload. Creating, saving, searching, reopening and deleting long notes were rechecked in both densities, including named deletion and cancellation. A failed preference write reports that the setting applies only to the current visit while preserving the draft.
