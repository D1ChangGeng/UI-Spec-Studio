# Leafnote: typography and density update

Leafnote now has a visible **Density** preference in the shared header, with **Comfortable** and **Compact** modes. The preference applies to both the collection and editor and survives reload.

## What changed

- **Clearer reading roles:** a stronger title, explicit context label, more readable metadata and a bounded prose measure.
- **Better long-note rhythm:** Comfortable uses larger body text and more generous spacing; Compact fits more notes and prose while retaining the same hierarchy.
- **Chinese and English treatments:** natural-case English labels, untracked Chinese labels, and increased Chinese title leading.
- **Narrow-screen controls:** Density has its own mobile header row, keeping its label and current value visible.
- **State preservation:** the selected note and unsaved draft remain intact through density, language and theme changes. The stored draft and density also survive reload.
