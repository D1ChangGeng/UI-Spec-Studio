## Meridian adaptation

The supplied native reference's coordinated conversation relationships are retained
in Meridian: the 48rem shared measure, independent reading region and persistent
composer, 28px composer contour, 22px bounded right-aligned user messages, and
state-prioritized send/stop controls. The empty-state composition is adapted to an
editorial title and prompt cards; warm colors, local history, source dock,
streaming state, persistence and filename chips are project-authored. This is a
cross-stack design/technique transfer, not integration of assistant-ui React.

`tokens.css` is copied from the supplied reference. Meridian uses the retained
Slate 11 values for secondary text in both themes; the other Slate scale values
remain available but are not claimed as active design contributions.
