# Source reading now stays connected to the arriving answer

Meridian now makes the citation relationship explicit while a long answer is still arriving. Opening a citation marks its answer paragraph and the referenced passage in the source reader. The existing paper palette, green accents, serif headings, and shared reading/composer proportions are retained.

[Open Meridian locally](http://127.0.0.1:8767/app/) · [Source entry](sandbox:/workspace/scratch/43b440780c87/granularity-cells/T2-p7/app/index.html)

## What changed

- **A visible connection:** the answer paragraph and source passage share a green highlight. **Show paragraph** takes you to the linked passage; the last inspected paragraph stays identifiable after closing.
- **Reading stays under your control:** opening a source pauses automatic following. Scrolling back remains stable while output arrives. **Back to latest** explicitly resumes following, including with the desktop panel open.
- **Controls remain available:** the source reader has a persistent latest-answer control for **Stop**, **Continue**, or **Retry**. You can keep reading the source while stopping or recovering the response.
- **A focused phone view:** the source opens in a native modal with an explicit **← Answer** return. Source text scrolls between the fixed return and response controls. The conversation and draft remain in place underneath.
- **Reliable return:** closing restores focus to the citation. If retry has replaced that citation, focus returns to its current replacement or the composer. A growing desktop draft stays reachable beside the source reader.

## Limits

The source passages remain deterministic synthetic notes. Real citation ranges, retrieval, and file parsing are outside this local demo. Retry restarts the latest answer; a linked paragraph that has not yet reappeared cannot be jumped to until it arrives. On phones, the modal deliberately focuses source reading; editing resumes after returning to the answer.

Physical mobile keyboard behavior, assistive technology, Safari/Firefox, and continuous-video timing remain unverified. Nothing was published or deployed.
