# Third-party material

Meridian retention note: the notices below came with the supplied reference
project. Meridian adopts the assistant-ui-derived composition/CSS relationships
identified in SOURCES.md. It does not ship the reference's tokens.css or use
Radix color values. The Radix notice below is retained as historical source
provenance, not as a claim of a Meridian runtime dependency.

`tokens.css` adapts the sRGB Slate/Slate Dark values from Radix Colors at
commit `dbdb85470547c7d34b9001f48fddb08ded335979`, `src/light.ts` and `src/dark.ts`.
The Slate role mappings and fixture behavior are project-authored. The page-level
layout adaptation is separately identified below.
