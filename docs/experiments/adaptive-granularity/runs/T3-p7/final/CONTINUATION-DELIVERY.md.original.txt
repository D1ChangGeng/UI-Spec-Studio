# Fieldnote now has a collection

Fieldnote now holds several saved note cards and opens each one in the existing two-sided editor. **Each note keeps its own identity, draft and pending save.** Returning to the collection restores its scroll position, highlights the card you just edited and returns keyboard focus to that card.

Open the local app at [http://127.0.0.1:8737](http://127.0.0.1:8737). [Run instructions](sandbox:/workspace/scratch/43b440780c87/granularity-cells/T3-p7/app/README.md)

## What works

- **A real collection:** create multiple notes, distinguish them by stable note numbers and content, and reopen any card. New installations start with an honest empty state. Existing single-note data carries forward.
- **Prepared navigation:** the draft and saved face are filled before opening. Rapid navigation follows the latest requested note, and returning takes the card back to its collection position.
- **Independent drafts and saves:** writing, switching cards and returning preserves in-tab drafts. A save that finishes after navigation updates its own note and does not turn or replace the newly opened card.
- **Truthful persistence and recovery:** successful saves reach native browser storage. Failed writes retain the prior saved collection and the editable draft; the collection marks that card and provides a route back to retry. Read failures do not masquerade as an empty collection.
- **Continuity:** the paper surface, original save-confirmed flip, long-note scrolling, keyboard selection/focus behavior, light/dark styling and reduced-motion equivalent remain part of the integrated task.

Saved notes survive reload. Unsaved drafts are explicitly marked as belonging to the current tab and remain available while that page stays open. There is no cloud sync.
