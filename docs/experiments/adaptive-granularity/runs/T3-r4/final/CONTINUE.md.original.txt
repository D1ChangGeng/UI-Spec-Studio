# Continue Fieldnote with a collection

Extend the same product to hold several saved note cards and open one for editing.
Returning to the collection should preserve the edited card's identity and the
person's orientation. Prepare needed content before the transition so rapid
navigation does not reveal an empty back face. Include an honest empty state and
safe recovery when local persistence fails. Preserve the initial card's useful
visual/motion relationships, reduced-motion equivalent and keyboard behavior.
Check repeated opening, editing, saving, returning and switching among long/short
notes. Show the integrated result and distinguish actual observed motion from
behavior assertions or merely captured media.
