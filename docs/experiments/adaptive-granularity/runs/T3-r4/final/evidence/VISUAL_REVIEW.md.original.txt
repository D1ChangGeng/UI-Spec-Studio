## Limits

Video was recorded by the browser suite but was not played back in a media viewer. No normal-speed playback or real-device smoothness claim is made. Capture brackets and order are in the JSON reports; screenshot overhead changes cadence. No screen-reader session, physical touch device, mobile virtual keyboard, Safari, Firefox, or field-performance measurement was available in this run. CJK was visually reviewed in the pinned local font environment, not across device font stacks. User acceptance remains pending.
