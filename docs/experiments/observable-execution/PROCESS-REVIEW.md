The independent audit identifies an important limit in A/C's checks: target

reason not to infer final quality from early disclosure or larger assertion counts.

The original comparative endpoints stay frozen. C receives a separately identified
post-review continuation within the same design responsibility, with additional
feedback and time. Its repair is not a replacement comparison result or evidence
of C's original superiority.

## Evidence boundaries

The source snapshots are captured when each message is recorded. Referenced media
are preserved with participant evidence at freeze time; the recorder does not
separately timestamp or hash every media file at message emission. Distinct trial,
first-product and final filenames help inspection, but source-time identity is
stronger than any claim about immutable early media. Human viewing and natural-speed
perceptual review must not be inferred from the availability of a link or recording.
The communication comparison covers deliberate outward messages, not every
tool-result surface that a host may also expose.

All supplied methods already contain substantial research, reuse and verification
guidance. One execution in each cell cannot isolate sampling variation, instruction
length, common relay overhead or shared-machine effects. The comparison can show
specific changes in visibility and real work; it cannot prove that C generally
outperforms A/B in final UI quality.
