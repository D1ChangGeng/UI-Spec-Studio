# UI Spec Studio 1.10.0

## Observe as people see, then apply what the design reveals

This release turns human-centered visual inspection into an explicit professional
method. Begin at intended viewing scale with the whole impression, then examine
composition, functional regions and consequential component or asset details.
Return to the complete interface and task after close inspection or a change.

Nine flexible perceptual lenses support hierarchy, space, typography, color,
material, shape, assets, movement and system identity. Select the relationships
that matter to the current object. Explain craft through purpose, optical precision,
coherent language, suitable expression and stability under real content and states.

Connect a visible fact to its likely perceptual effect, explanatory relationship
or hypothesis, current-product fit, mature-resource choice and actual recheck.
Source demos, local trials and assembled journeys keep their own evidence. Details
are judged beside their neighbors; movement returns to the full episode after
diagnostic examination. Collected media, actual viewing, engineering measurements
and observed user response remain distinct.

The method strengthens actual adoption and focused adaptation of mature work.
The eight common references, `init → iterate → finish`, optional `shape`, existing
host authority and lightweight records remain. No fixed aesthetic score, capture
quota or additional installed runtime dependency is introduced.
