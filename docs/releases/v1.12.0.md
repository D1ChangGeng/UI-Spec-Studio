# UI Spec Studio 1.12.0

Design research now starts with a revisable understanding of the experience users
expect. It connects product understanding, evidence planning, sustained exploration,
meaningful candidate comparison and mature-resource adoption to the actual result.

## Design behavior

- Expand a product analogy into tasks, working objects, information relationships,
  visual qualities and continuous use. Keep current-user intent, target-user
  experience and actual product evidence distinct.
- Plan text, visual and behavioral evidence around the experience structure.
  Retain relevant version, platform, role and viewing conditions.
- Scale exploration by design impact, uncertainty and potential improvement.
  Follow discoveries across sources, product categories, components and assets;
  investigate alternatives capable of changing an initial preference.
- Compare materially different candidates under useful, comparable conditions.
  Link observed differences to a selection, next research action or local trial.
- Continue research for consequential gaps; trial supported candidates in current
  conditions; expand implementation when important fit is demonstrated.
- Preserve the connection from target quality to original demonstration, local
  trial, adopted implementation and assembled experience. Use legitimate
  alternatives and focused user assistance to obtain missing material.

## Evidence and delivery

Evidence is judged through coverage, detail, time, relevance and reliability.
Source descriptions, actual observation, professional judgment, runtime checks
and owner acceptance retain their own scope. Verification covers intended tasks,
visual maturity, independent expression and engineering reliability.

The bilingual README and [experience research guide](https://github.com/D1ChangGeng/UI-Spec-Studio/blob/v1.12.0/docs/EXPERIENCE-RESEARCH.md)
explain the complete method. The eight common references, mature-implementation
default, optional records and `init → iterate → finish` remain the working
foundation. Sustained exploration is a capability guided by decision value;
individual tasks determine its appropriate depth.

The [version review](https://github.com/D1ChangGeng/UI-Spec-Studio/blob/v1.12.0/docs/REVIEW-v1.12.0.md) records executed release checks, browser
observations and agent applications with their actual artifacts and conditions.
The [assurance model](https://github.com/D1ChangGeng/UI-Spec-Studio/blob/v1.12.0/docs/EVALUATION.md) explains what each kind of evidence can
establish.
