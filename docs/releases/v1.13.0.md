# UI Spec Studio 1.13.0 — Historical content integrity

Review published history from v1.1.0 through v1.12.1 for implicit shortening
and weakened design instruction. The core change is **knowledge preservation
before text economy**.

## Changes

- Identify the fixed 128 KiB (v1.5) and 144 KiB (v1.6–v1.12) common-reference
  release gates, distinguishing package growth from limited required
  professional-reference growth.
- Restore historical specificity in product brief interpretation, positive
  craft targets, mature composition, detailed iteration, source acquisition,
  five reuse operations, concurrent motion, and perceptual review.
- Remove the arbitrary aggregate reference-byte gate and rigid entry-line
  validation condition. Retain the eight-reference full-reading contract
  and applicable host/tool boundaries.
- Establish a permanent [content integrity procedure](https://github.com/D1ChangGeng/UI-Spec-Studio/blob/v1.13.0/docs/CONTENT-INTEGRITY.md),
  a tagged lineage ledger and automated structural regression checks.
- Keep later improvements, including experience-grounded evidence planning,
  high-intensity source exploration, mature-resource adoption and actual
  verification, alongside the restored knowledge.

The [full historical audit](https://github.com/D1ChangGeng/UI-Spec-Studio/blob/v1.13.0/docs/REVIEW-v1.13.0.md)
explains what was compressed, what survived and what was restored. Automated
checks verify their declared mechanics; they do not certify model design quality
or human approval.
