## Sustainable multi-round development

Version 1.21.0 strengthens the `ui-spec` iterative loop (`init`, optional `shape`, `iterate`, `audit`, `finish`) for long-lived, multi-session, multi-engineer brownfield product development across five connected responsibilities:

1. **Cross-session continuation and anti-relitigation:** Re-entry reconciles the `Overall Product Goal`, `Current Design Model` and live `Current Product UI` so fresh host sessions inherit settled foundations and scoped approvals without redundant discovery, while reopening claims only when user intent, rendered evidence or operating conditions change. Completed increments and interrupted mid-SPEC handoffs leave explicit runnable entry paths, per-scope status facts and next actions.
2. **Living multi-SPEC horizon and safe retirement:** Formal increments are governed as an evolving horizon ordered by user value, dependencies, shared-foundation leverage and risk. Superseded, consolidated and retired SPECs or surfaces account for where valid user task paths and unresolved quality obligations move so obsolete entry points do not linger.
3. **Brownfield engineering and contract drift reconciliation:** Between-round code commits, backend schema/permission updates, emergency UI hotfixes and requirement pivots are diagnosed by causal layer—preserving compatible refactors and functional bug fixes while adopting authoritative service facts and repairing visual craft or default-deny provenance on top of live implementations.
4. **Staged shared-system migrations and proportional regression:** Multi-increment upgrades to typography, semantic tokens, layout shells or mature component families prove fit on a representative anchor slice first, establish explicit coexistence bridges under default-deny W1–W3 rules, track legacy decommission, and focus regression on the changed slice, migration seams and high-risk extremum consumers rather than `O(entire app)` re-audits.
5. **Bounded `O(active product)` compaction and debt anti-evaporation:** Superseded candidate comparisons, closed intermediate trials and retired surface history compact into `Meaningful Revisions` and cold-linked `Evidence Archive` records while keeping four explicit obligation classes—visual/craft, provisional probe/bridge, integration/contract and verification-scope debt—visible until verified closed or retired with cause.
