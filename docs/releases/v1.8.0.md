# UI Spec Studio 1.8.0

Product UI Experience Alignment makes the current product's intended experience
the organizing goal of design work. A named product is expanded into relevant
pages, flows and qualities; a functional brief prompts proactive discovery of
suitable patterns, design systems and implementations.

Active Visual Exploration & Design Reconnaissance turns product understanding
into research questions and concrete browsing actions. Agents follow useful
clues through playback, scrolling, state changes and variant comparison, covering
consequential composition, states and relationships. Research converges through
adequate evidence and a viable adaptation path.

Observations identify their actual subject: the hosting page, media player,
demonstrated application or content inside it. Existing demonstrations and active
interaction recordings answer different questions. Capture, actual inspection and
sufficient evidence for a dynamic conclusion remain distinct.

Reuse connects inherited design decisions with reusable implementation. Design
references and implementation resources can come from different sources; the
selected work must carry the relationship the current product needs. Runnable
slices revisit the original research questions under representative content,
viewing and interaction conditions, before review expands to the whole product.

The eight common references, optional records and established
`init → iterate → finish` path retain their responsibilities. Detailed methods
remain at their responsible source. The bilingual README and
[product alignment guide](https://github.com/D1ChangGeng/UI-Spec-Studio/blob/v1.8.0/docs/PRODUCT-ALIGNMENT.md)
explain the connected workflow.

Validation evidence is recorded in the
[version review](https://github.com/D1ChangGeng/UI-Spec-Studio/blob/v1.8.0/docs/REVIEW-v1.8.0.md)
and the release workflow. Package, browser, observed design and agent-application
results each retain their exercised scope.
