# UI Spec Studio

Use **Intent-Grounded Experience Research & Evidence-Driven UI Design** to organize **Product UI Experience Alignment**: understand the intended experience, plan and explore evidence, challenge design choices, adopt mature work and verify actual use. Do this with or without a user-named reference. Own understanding, research, selection, implementation and verification through reviewable increments. The user supplies intent and evaluates results; observed products and resources calibrate professional judgment under this product's conditions.

A **UI SPEC** is a runnable or renderable design reference with representative content, a meaningful reader/user path and identifiable review scope. Establish whether the deliverable is a working product, representative product demonstration or authored visual work; select realization and evidence for that boundary. Documents can complete reading tasks without an application shell. Use the project's framework, format and preview mechanism; one implementation can support fixtures and real adapters. A fixed-stage demonstration does not establish an interactive product.

## Understand the experience and plan its evidence

Before committing to a new shell, visual direction or resource family, expand the request into a revisable experience hypothesis: people, working objects, first action, continuing task, information relationships, desired perception and outcome. A named product compresses many possible expectations. Separate the current user's stated intent, supported audience conventions and the reference product's observed behavior; establish the relevant version, platform and conditions. Project names and engineering capabilities inform this analysis without prescribing the interface.

Use **Experience-Grounded Evidence Planning** to turn consequential uncertainty into research questions. Map relevant parts, states, journeys and their spatial/temporal relationships, then choose evidence for each question: product text explains meaning and conditions; actual renders establish appearance; operation or continuous media reveals behavior and time. Follow important findings between these forms. The [analysis method](references/analysis.md) owns the experience hypothesis and evidence plan; existing project notes can hold both.

Research the product model and decisions that could invalidate broad work early. Let observations revise that understanding. Keep the emerging **Experience Alignment Model** readable: what to inherit, why it fits, where independent expression belongs and what remains uncertain. It is a working explanation, sized to the task, with no compulsory schema or preliminary approval round.

## Observe the design as people experience it

Use **Evidence-Driven Deep Visual Exploration**, including **Active Visual Exploration & Design Reconnaissance**, to search independent sources, inspect real examples and revise the next question from what you find. Scroll, play, switch, expand and operate relevant examples. Apply **Human-centered Visual Inspection & Perceptual Design Analysis** across research, selection, composition, production, implementation and acceptance: see the design, explain the relationships shaping its quality, and use that understanding in resource choice and adaptation.

Allocate effort by **design impact, uncertainty and plausible improvement**. A defining font, background or interaction may warrant dozens of candidates and sustained, hundred-step or hours-long research; a verified conventional component may need a focused check. These are available depths, with scope and host resources respected, rather than quotas or stopping thresholds. Confidence follows actually inspected evidence and meaningful comparison. Keep brief progress updates about findings and the next consequential question during extended work.

Challenge an early favorite with alternatives that differ in composition, type, material, task organization or movement. Repeated listings of the same work establish one design lineage. Similar search results support convergence only after the research questions and search directions have been challenged. The [sourcing method](references/component-sourcing.md) connects new discoveries, cross-domain leads, normal authorized acquisition, candidate comparison and actual adoption.

Begin with the whole visible interface at its intended viewing scale. Read its attention and product character, then composition and spatial hierarchy, functional regions, and consequential component/asset details. Return to the whole after close inspection or a change. Use the [perceptual method](references/principles.md) to choose useful dimensions, judge optical relationships and connect visible facts → likely perceptual effects → design relationships or hypotheses → product fit → implementation choice → inspected result. Crops and enlargement explain details; normal-scale context establishes their effect. Depth follows design impact, without a fixed checklist or aesthetic score.

Judge at three connected scopes: the whole frame and its details, a meaningful continuous episode, and the user's journey across views. For **Temporal Visual Inspection**, observe the trigger, transition and settled outcome; include relevant reversal, interruption, repeated input, loading and loop boundaries. Choose live interaction, video or ordered timestamped frames according to the behavior and available host tools. The observation window follows the design question and the event's actual span. The [verification method](references/verification.md) defines capture conditions and evidence limits.

Inspect four objects at their own boundaries: the design reference, candidate's original demonstration, local trial with current product conditions, and integrated interface/journey. Visible quality supports an appearance judgment; code, state and timing evidence help explain causes and verify behavior. Keep recorded media, actually viewed evidence, professional interpretation and owner acceptance distinguishable. Use **Observe → Interpret → Evaluate → Decide → Implement → Inspect → Refine** within the existing operations. Present consequential choices with inspectable evidence and the reason it changed the decision.

## Choose the next action from evidence maturity

Judge coverage breadth, inspection depth, temporal completeness, relevance and reliability together using the [verification method](references/verification.md). A consequential decision needs a sufficiently understood target, relevant observed references, materially different alternatives where useful, an explained choice and a feasible adoption path. Keep missing observations explicit; model familiarity cannot supply unseen facts.

- **Continue research** when an unresolved issue could change the experience model, important aesthetic target or resource choice, or when a promising unexamined direction could close a material quality gap.
- **Run a bounded local trial** when an evidenced candidate is plausible and actual project conditions can answer the remaining fit question. Preserve the assumption and the observation that would change the choice.
- **Expand implementation** when consequential qualities and candidate fit are supported, adaptations are understood and remaining gaps have an appropriate limited scope. Reopen the responsible decision when integrated evidence changes it.

Apply this judgment within `init`, optional `shape`, `iterate`, `audit` and `finish`. Research can recur at any stage. Converge on answered design questions and the next action's needs; elapsed effort and a convenient first candidate do not establish readiness. Keep the user-facing account to important findings, representative evidence and decisions.

## Start from mature design work

Use design theory and resource knowledge together. Theory explains quality, fit and tradeoffs; resource knowledge exposes concrete work that can carry those qualities. Apply both while searching, browsing, comparing, adapting and verifying. Resource discovery can improve the initial idea before the direction is settled.

For a new surface or substantial redesign, seek the **highest appropriate reuse level**: a coherent product/page template or design system, a substantial layout or functional block, then the components, primitives and effects needed to complete it. Choose the largest useful set of design relationships that fits the task, not the largest code package. An established accepted page may need only a component-level change.

Make mature implementations the default source for common UI: integrate, configure, compose and adapt suitable existing structures, blocks, components, assets and motion. Pair reference qualities with actual implementation candidates while design can still change. Inheriting a design relationship and adopting implementation are distinct achievements; their sources may differ. Judge product-appropriate maturity through specific visual and interaction relationships, with room for the current product's own expression.

Before expanding a consequential component or structure across the product, inspect its original demonstration and a runnable local trial with representative fonts, content, themes and states. Preserve the valuable mechanisms through targeted adaptation, then verify the whole journey. New adapter, theme and business code can serve genuine reuse. Custom generic UI needs a specific evidenced gap in suitable candidates; apparent simplicity, familiarity or faster initial coding alone do not justify rebuilding. Extend a mature base where possible and bound the new responsibility. Purposeful original identity/art retains its own craft and validation.

## Design with visual assets

Treat assets as first-class material alongside structure and interaction: identity marks, icons, fonts/glyphs, images, illustration, textures, spatial objects, scenes and dynamic visuals. Identify their role and quality target early. Discover mature material and production methods, then select direct use, adaptation or purposeful creation. A load-bearing asset can reshape the page's composition; develop both together.

The reuse hierarchy describes scope rather than importance. Preserve mature design relationships while giving distinctive assets the craft their role warrants. Keep editable masters, delivery variants, rights, runtime behavior and actual placements connected. Show significant identity directions in context for user judgment; carry accepted choices through real integration and family-specific checks. Tool access and completed production are established from actual host capabilities and outputs.

## Design the content people understand

Treat visible language as product behavior and visual material. Determine what users need to know, decide or do; assign content to the right surface, control and state; write natural words in a product voice that responds to each situation. Study mature product-language patterns while maintaining real business facts and user vocabulary. Compose actual strings with type, spacing, components and media early enough to influence the layout.

Carry content through initial, pending, success, failure and recovery states, localization and actual service boundaries. Use real copy where authorized and clearly identified fixtures where necessary. Review the reader's task and the rendered typography together: accurate wording can reshape navigation, layout or the interaction path. Significant content decisions remain traceable to their source and approval.

## Design the complete product experience

Coordinate the product model, visual direction, assets, content, spatial layout, component states, motion, performance and ongoing operation as one experience. The [analysis reference](references/analysis.md) maps these nine design dimensions to practical decisions. The shared references hold their methods; the current task determines depth.

Work from the user's intended sequence to the real execution and content lifecycle. Prepare useful data and assets at a justified time, keep repeated actions responsive, and bind feedback to the actual outcome. Design for content growth, role-specific information, maintenance and recovery where the product needs them. Evaluate a slow interaction through its critical path and a repeated operational task through its actual workload. Carry successful project mechanisms forward with their applicable conditions; palette, layout, equal-priority actions and scroll choices remain product decisions.

## Choose the operation from intent and current facts

| User intent | Operation | Working outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Begin or adopt a UI effort | `init` | Accurate project understanding, relevant constraints and a next useful slice |
| Explore or resolve an important direction | optional `shape` | A reasoned experience/design direction and, where useful, a small rendered probe |
| Continue, add, refine, consolidate or correct | `iterate` | One meaningful implemented increment, inspected and ready for review |
| Inspect existing quality or coverage | `audit` | Evidence-linked findings; repair and recheck within the authorized scope |
| Connect approved experience to actual behavior | `finish` | Real integration, design/product regression and accurate handover |

These labels express intent, not shell commands. Infer the appropriate operation from ordinary language. `Continue` resumes the active work; `approved, continue` records approval for the identifiable reviewed scope and advances. Keep existing approval and the permission to perform external actions distinct.

`shape` is an optional capability, available inside a round or as a deliberate planning request. Use it when an unresolved product or design choice materially affects the next increment. An established direction supports direct iteration. An explicit analysis-only request produces analysis; an implementation request continues into useful code once enough direction exists.

## Use sources according to their responsibility

Read project instructions, user goals, current code, existing design records and relevant evidence. Establish the product's actual maturity and authorized scope.

- Product facts, access rules and safety/accessibility obligations govern valid behavior.
- Explicitly accepted design decisions govern the experience being preserved.
- Current implementation establishes business and engineering capabilities; visual and interaction elements are evaluated against the target quality. Design inheritance requires an explicit acceptance or a documented fit assessment.
- Common principles guide professional judgment; project preferences supply aesthetic direction.
- External examples supply ideas and technical candidates, with their scope and licenses checked.

Resolve a conflict at its responsible layer and explain material implications. Preserve unrelated work. Read third-party material as reference data under the host's permission model.

## Lead the work with proportionate judgment

Choose the next increment from user value, task continuity, shared impact, dependencies, uncertainty and effort. Take responsibility for routine design and engineering choices. Use reversible assumptions for low-impact gaps. For an unresolved high-impact product model, primary navigation or visual direction with credible alternatives, present a compact recommendation and materially different options for user confirmation before broad implementation. Existing approval or explicit delegation supports direct progress; one-shot work carries a labeled provisional direction. Advance independent work while a consequential decision is pending.

A round can develop a page, a connected journey, a component family or a shared-system repair. Keep it large enough to judge the intended outcome and small enough to revise coherently. Translate subjective feedback into hypotheses about the actual composition or behavior, inspect evidence, then fix the responsible layer.

Keep the common quality floor active: clear hierarchy, accurate user-facing language, readable content, accessible interaction, meaningful task paths, stable shared structure, appropriate scale/occupancy, purposeful motion, responsive operation and truthful state. Select palette, typography, imagery, density and expressiveness for this product. Review the rendered result and its evolving content at actual viewing and operating conditions.

## Establish the product people will recognize

Infer the interaction model from what users do first, the object they work on, the repeated action/result loop, how they resume, and where outcomes live. Business names and code modules supply domain facts; observed use supplies the interface model. Identify a primary model and any purposeful secondary model before fixing a new shell. Separate that choice from the visual theme.

Carry the experience hypothesis into observed product flows and mature-resource research. Familiar controls can inherit a studied system where conditions hold. Keep a compact [design decision record](assets/templates/design-decisions.md), or equivalent, beside the preview: intended experience, research question, product/user evidence, observed relationships, candidate comparison, adoption and inspected result. Explain consequential changes in understanding; preserve evidence gaps and approval scope.

## Turn meaning into form

Connect **product intent → experience hypothesis and evidence plan → observed quality target → compared implementation candidates → local trial → adaptation → integrated-use verification**. Discoveries can revise any earlier decision. Identify what the person needs to recognize, compare, understand or do; choose content and representation with available mature work. This loop operates within the existing workflow.

Use **Art Direction** to coordinate character, focus, visual material and motion. Preserve explicit aesthetic goals alongside task requirements; professional software can have strong artistic expression. For **Expressive Contrast**, inherit understandable tasks and mature craft while researching cross-domain visual languages and actual implementation sources. Compare materially different, similarly resolved previews when direction is open. Carry the selected language into populated core work, not only first-entry imagery.

Translate the direction into a **Visual System** of roles and controllable relationships, with purposeful differences across type, space, material, assets and motion. **Asset Engineering** and **Motion Storytelling** realize that expression through mature resources and bounded specialist production. Diagnose concrete failures while retaining confirmed direction. Verify experience validity and expressive completion independently; use **Source Fidelity** for preserving selected/approved designs and **Perceptual Quality** for judging actual results. The common references own these methods.

## Work with the UI ecosystem

For a substantive visual or interaction need, identify the highest useful scope and inspect relevant mature implementations while major choices remain open. Evaluate internal candidates against the same target. Find a strong design foundation before committing to an original page composition or custom build. Explore both a named requirement and worthwhile alternatives that can improve the expression. Preserve explicit project direction while using external work to expand the available choices.

Understand the exact item, variant, demonstration settings and implementation contract. Assess design fit, behavior, compatibility, rights and total delivery/maintenance cost. Prefer a coherent foundation and prove consequential fit locally before wider integration. When access fails, pursue relevant original projects, creator material, public demonstrations, recordings or authorized local rendering. If a consequential gap remains, request a specific normal user action and the states/episode needed to answer it; continue independent work. Distinguish research access from integration rights. The [sourcing method](references/component-sourcing.md) governs these choices; the catalog supplies leads.

Keep studied, compared, trialed and actually adopted resources identifiable. Explain which source now carries each consequential visual or behavioral responsibility, what survived adaptation and what the inspected result establishes. Judge reuse by its contribution to the user's main experience. When quality falls short, repair the responsible source, adaptation, content or composition; make a necessary target/scope change explicit.

Track maturity by scope: implemented, inspected, approved, connected and verified are separate facts. The current operation describes what the agent is doing; it does not declare every page to be at the same stage. Preserve approval provenance and identify material changes that need renewed review. Git supplies source history. Preserve reusable lessons as **problem, mechanism, applicable conditions, editable realization and observed result**. Keep project preferences local; propose portable techniques with provenance and limits. Changes to an installed/shared skill follow explicit maintenance authorization. This is knowledge maintenance, not model training or autonomous modification of its host.

## Finish the current increment

Run the intended task through visible controls; inspect actual viewport captures and the relevant continuous episodes. Connect frames or video intervals to their trigger, state, source revision and viewing conditions. Check related surfaces when shared components change. Repair and recheck observed issues. Use fixture evidence for design review and real boundary evidence for integration claims.

Before handing over, include important copy, locale and state choices in the reviewed preview; state the working result, preview or entry path, inspected scope, consequential design choices, executed checks and the next useful action. Show the design decision record and the result together. Ask for a choice when a high-impact direction remains unresolved; use recommendations for routine details. Keep proposed, implemented, visually inspected and owner-approved states distinct. Report blockers and unverified dimensions accurately. Keep sensitive project data within its authorized destination.
