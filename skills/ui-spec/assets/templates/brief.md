# UI SPEC project brief

## Product and people

Purpose, audience, use context, primary tasks and observable success.

## Current project

Repository/frontend, existing components, run/test commands, actual product maturity,
source of product facts and authorized change scope.

## Accepted direction

Visual identity, typography/language coverage, palette roles, density, composition,
spacing, imagery, motion and links to the existing design-system document.

## Page families and journeys

Responsibility, shared shell, entry, next actions, relevant roles and recovery.
Keep detailed coverage in the manifest or the project's existing equivalent.

## Component decisions

Role | source/component | checked URL/version/date | selection reason | adaptation/license

## Verification scope

Viewports, supported variants, critical states, representative data, browser access,
privacy of evidence and the reason for any scoped exclusion.

## Direction and open decisions

Binding user preferences, agent-selected reversible assumptions, material unresolved
facts and the smallest next observation that could change the design.

## Current round

Selected outcome, why now, accepted assumptions, implemented result, review status,
approval reference and next meaningful action.

## Scope continuity

Identify implemented, inspected, approved, connected and verified scopes separately.
Record the source of approval and what the next round should inherit. The active
operation describes current work; individual scopes can have different maturity.
