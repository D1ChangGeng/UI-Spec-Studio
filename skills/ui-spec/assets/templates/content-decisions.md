# Content design decisions

## User, surface and purpose

User's current task, what they know on arrival, what they need to understand and the meaningful next action. Source of accepted product direction and applicable brand voice/terminology.

## Decision points and content placement

| Surface/state / content key | User question and content role | Source fact or owner | Approved or proposed copy by locale | Display condition and typography role | Behavior and evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use rows for consequential navigation, purchase/access terms, key state and error messages, or localization choices that change layout. Share keys where meaning and grammatical context agree. Record authoritative completion conditions, partial results or unknown outcomes when they affect the next action. Keep routine copy near its implementation and existing project records.

## Language and style

Stable object and action names, voice characteristics, scenario tone, punctuation and term choices. Include complete localized messages, variable meanings and accessible names when relevant. Distinguish local style preferences from business wording and accessibility requirements.

## Source, roles and maintenance

For content that changes over time, identify its source and editor, shared template and local override behavior, and how the effective result appears to its audience. Record role-specific wording, language fallbacks and draft/saved/published meaning only where the product needs them. Link repeated facts to their authority and identify placements or translations affected by a change.

## Verification and continuation

Representative short/long strings, supported languages/themes/viewports, states and task paths reviewed; source revision and actual approval scope. Cover visible and accessible labels, feedback placement, recovery and content updates as relevant. Distinguish mechanical checks, editorial observations and real-user task evidence. Record facts still awaiting authoritative confirmation and the next relevant check.
