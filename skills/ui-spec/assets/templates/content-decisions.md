# Content design decisions

## User, surface and purpose

User's current task, what they know on arrival, what they need to understand and the meaningful next action. Source of accepted product direction and applicable brand voice/terminology.

## Decision points and content placement

| Surface/state / content key | User question and content role | Source fact or owner | Approved or proposed copy by locale | Display condition and typography role | Behavior and evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use rows for consequential navigation, purchase/access terms, key state and error messages, or localization choices that change layout. Share keys across the product where they represent one meaning. Routine copy remains in component code and tests.

## Language and style

Stable object and action names, voice characteristics, scenario tone, punctuation and term choices. Distinguish local style preferences from business wording and accessibility requirements.

## Verification and continuation

Representative short/long strings, supported languages/themes/viewports, states and task paths reviewed; source revision and actual approval scope. Record facts still awaiting authoritative confirmation and the next relevant check.
