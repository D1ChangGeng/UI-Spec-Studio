# UI review

## Scope and condition

Design question; source resource, assembled composition or delivered runtime;
routes/states, viewport, browser, theme, locale, data/font readiness, revision and
actual entry path. Retain the conditions needed to interpret and repeat this review.

## Observation

Whole-frame hierarchy, core scale and occupancy, optical balance, whitespace,
readability, shared anchors, scroll ownership and reachable controls.
Link the viewed frame or interval to the visible relationship, its likely effect
on perception, and the product goal. Record useful strengths as well as problems:
an observation can justify adopting or preserving a design.

## Temporal episode

When quality changes over time, name the object, starting context, trigger,
meaningful progression and settled result or stopping condition. Explain why the
window covers the question; include relevant reversal, interruption, repeated
input, loop seam, delayed result or connected return.

Link recordings or ordered frames to actual event times and source revision.
Keep video size, frame order, capture-time brackets/cadence, gaps and time-control
settings where they affect interpretation. Identify the intervals actually viewed
and the useful full-size frames. Distinguish natural-speed playback, sampled
frames and controlled-time diagnosis; each supports its stated observation.

## Interaction

Task steps, focus/input/selection retention, transitions, error/retry and result.

## Findings and repair

Observed relationship and evidence → perceptual/task effect → cause to investigate
→ design choice and responsible layer → changed result and recheck.

Keep visual observation, professional interpretation and verified cause distinct.
Compare equivalent conditions and meaningful event phases. Recheck the affected
interval and connected path after changing a shared component, asset or state owner.

## Result

Captured evidence; actually inspected frames/intervals; executed interaction and
engineering checks; current result; actual user approval and remaining conditions.
Provide inspectable candidates or before/after results when they explain an
important choice. Existing project notes can carry this record at a useful depth.

## Content review

Identify the current task question, intended message, source fact, actual wording/state, typography/layout result, locale coverage and observed behavior. Scope editorial judgment, user comprehension and business approval separately.

## Runtime and maintenance observations

When relevant, record input feedback, usable result and confirmed completion under
stated device/network/data conditions; motion and scroll continuity; preparation cost;
effective template/override content; batch outcomes and recovery. State the metric's
actual boundary and compare equivalent conditions. Link traces and runtime evidence.
