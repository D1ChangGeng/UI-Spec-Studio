# Delegation: main-agent authority and specialist collaboration

Use **Main-Agent-Centered Adaptive Research & Integration** when independent inquiry, bounded source integration, adversarial craft critique or complex episode capture can improve the current design decision. Auxiliary roles are optional capacity inside `init`, optional `shape`, `iterate`, `audit` and `finish`—never a second workflow, a design committee or a mandatory stage. Every delegated task remains internal work under the active formal SPEC and honors the eight-reference reading contract and [default-deny original construction](component-sourcing.md#default-deny-original-ui-construction) boundary.

## Main-agent authority and auxiliary specialist scope

The main agent remains the active designer, decision-maker and whole-product integration owner. Never transfer any of the following responsibilities to an auxiliary role:

- **Overall Product Goal and Formal UI SPEC ownership** — deciding scope, repartitioning increments or declaring a SPEC complete.
- **Pivotal visual judgment and final mature-resource selection** — directly inspecting the evidence that governs defining visual, typographic, material or motion choices.
- **W1–W3 originality decisions and user authorization** — researcher confidence, source inaccessibility, license mismatch or trial failure never authorizes original visual construction.
- **Whole-product integration and quality acceptance** — verifying that assembled surfaces, shared anchors, typography, focus, scrolling and real behavior work coherently together.

Specialists investigate, critique, capture or integrate within explicit boundaries and return inspectable evidence. A subordinate's recommendation is an input to judgment, never the judgment itself.

## Choose when and how to delegate

Decide whether to delegate by weighing **design impact, evidence uncertainty, plausible craft improvement, independence of the inquiry and coordination cost** together. A routine adjustment inside an already inspected mature system rarely needs delegation; a small identity-bearing hero surface, typography pairing or signature motion episode can warrant sustained independent exploration. These factors guide professional judgment rather than a fixed score or minimum agent count.

Frame each delegation as a **complete, coherent question** that the specialist can pursue through its own search → observation → analysis → comparison loop. Do not split search, viewing and comparison into a rigid multi-agent assembly line. Protect coupled experience relationships—for example, composer growth, reading measure, scroll following and adjacent workspace panes should be studied together even when their implementation files differ. Multiple inquiries may overlap when they gather independent evidence or materially different alternatives; partition by design question or exploration direction rather than mechanically by website, page, DOM element or visual discipline.

Provide concise natural-language intent and authoritative project references instead of a compulsory delegation schema:

1. **Intended quality and product context** — what the user is doing, the target aesthetic/experience bar and existing constraints.
2. **Known facts and consequential unknown** — what is already established and what observation could confirm or overturn the next choice.
3. **Permitted scope and boundaries** — read-only research vs. allowed write modules, shared contracts, tools and real host permissions.

Stable role instructions already carry the professional method, so the main agent need not retype it. By default, proposed additional investigators return to the main agent rather than recursively spawning an unmanaged team. While an inquiry runs, advance **independent, supported work** (inspecting current content and dependencies, preparing a representative trial harness, verifying an established region or integrating already selected work). Never hand-build a provisional visual foundation to fill an open source gap merely to stay busy.

If a delegated agent or tool fails or is unavailable, keep the underlying quality responsibility visible: retry with a sharper question, switch to another authorized evidence route or investigate directly. Keep user-facing conversation output focused on consequential discoveries, resource choices, first actual results and remaining gaps—do not stream internal delegation chatter.

## Natural research delegation with ui_researcher

Invoke `ui_researcher` when wider independent discovery, deep visual reconnaissance, source tracing or cross-domain hunting can raise the ceiling of candidate quality. The researcher applies the same substantive discovery, real viewing, perceptual analysis, temporal inspection, comparison and source tracing as direct research, pivoting queries, ecosystems and scale as clues emerge (a page leading to its type foundry, an effect to its author's repository, a promising block to a complete design system).

Expect **multi-tier wide discovery** rather than stopping at the first plausible package:

- **Tier 1 (Taste & product benchmarks):** elite live products, award-caliber editorial/type galleries and documented design systems that calibrate hierarchy, luminance ladders, density and motion poise.
- **Tier 2 (High-craft component & section registries):** curated visual registries and block ecosystems (such as coss, Kokonut UI, Motion Primitives, 21st.dev, Tailark, tweakcn, Magic UI, Aceternity UI, React Bits, Vue Bits, Inspira UI and peer open ecosystems) evaluated for resolved visual completeness, not merely API ergonomics.
- **Tier 3 (Specialist engines & flagship OSS codebases):** proven interaction, layout, charting, 3D/WebGL, shader and motion libraries alongside well-crafted open-source applications demonstrating real integrated craft.
- **Tier 4 (Typography, icon & surface craft):** independent type foundries and optical glyph/icon/SVG craft sources (such as Fontshare, Fontsource, Phosphor, Tabler, fffuel and peer ecosystems) that anchor visual voice.

Actively **reject generic defaults** (stock Bootstrap, unstyled shadcn primitives, uniform card-box grids, cliché gradient blobs or unDraw-style stock illustrations) **as final visual surfaces** on craft-critical areas—keeping functional primitives distinct from resolved visual layers. Verify upstream maintenance, live demo vs. source code drift, exact item/variant identity, license terms and framework compatibility.

**Curate an entry point, not a final answer.** Return a compact synthesis in the parent context while preserving direct links to retrievable originals, screenshots and viewed intervals:

- **Concrete craft mechanics extracted:** spatial proportions, display/body/mono type pairing, luminance steps, hairline/shadow model, concentric radii and motion timing/easing alongside verified adoptable source links.
- **Independent lineages over duplicate listings:** preserve materially different alternatives, credible counterexamples and exceptional **reference-only** exemplars (explicitly separated from adoptable implementations, with adoptable routes capable of carrying their valuable relationships).
- **Honest evidence states:** distinguish discovered, downloaded, actually viewed, documented, inferred, trialed, adopted and verified facts; state observation conditions and unverified assumptions. A bounded unsuccessful search establishes investigated limits, not universal absence.

## Bounded deep-reuse integration with ui_integrator

Invoke `ui_integrator` only after the visual direction and mature foundation are selected and the change boundary is concrete. Before the integrator writes code, establish the exact **source/version/variant**, **protected craft relationships** (proportions, typography, material, states, motion), **allowed files/modules**, **shared interfaces**, **real data/state owners** and **observable checks**.

Within that boundary, the integrator executes genuine deep reuse rather than superficial copy-paste:

- **Source-preserving de-templating and token harmonization:** replace placeholder copy/themes with the project's canonical semantic tokens, font stack, luminance ladder and radius grammar while preserving the source's defining spatial, optical and interaction relationships.
- **Cascade and style isolation:** prevent Tailwind/reset/global-CSS collisions using clean cascade layers (`@layer`), scoped variables and proper wrapper geometry so glows, hairlines, clipping bounds and scroll containers survive integration.
- **Rendering-fidelity and ergonomic repairs:** fix concrete integration defects without degrading the source—such as flattening resting 3D transforms that blur crisp text, preserving mobile first-screen writing/reading room, enforcing `aria-modal` and focus return after dismissal/stop/save failure, and synchronizing mid-transition `inert`/`aria-hidden` with live geometry.
- **Faithful source-derived porting:** when explicitly assigned in the main agent's integration scope (for example, porting a verified visual component across frameworks from its inspected upstream source code under a compatible license, or translating a complete user-supplied design within its specified authority), retain exact optical, state and motion mechanics and verify against the source demo.

**Escalate unresolved design choices instead of improvising.** An unmade layout, visual-language, motion or source-selection decision is not a deterministic coding task. Return missing visual responsibilities or broken source fit to the main agent rather than inventing unsourced CSS or hand-rebuilding a mature component. Original visual responsibility still requires the exact authorized [W1–W3 scope](component-sourcing.md#closed-original-construction-allowlist).

**Enforce filesystem and contract safety.** Agree on shared types, token names and state owners before parallel writes, and assign non-overlapping files or isolated worktrees. Never assume distinct agent names imply isolated filesystems: verify the actual checkout, preserve unrelated user or agent edits and stop conflicting writes. Return exact modified files, adopted sources, checks run, viewed evidence and unresolved risks; never declare the formal SPEC complete.

## High-taste craft challenge with ui_challenger

Invoke `ui_challenger` when a consequential direction, resource choice or integrated draft risks premature convergence, aesthetic mediocrity or degraded craft. The challenger inspects both the claim and the rendered evidence to stress-test whether the current choice truly meets the product's taste bar:

- **Hunt generic defaults and AI-slop signatures:** diagnose unadapted template layouts, timid type ladders, flat surfaces lacking light/edge depth, muddy or harsh dark themes, competing accents and multi-source Frankenstein seams ([failure signatures](principles.md#diagnostic-generic-ui-and-ai-slop-failure-signatures)).
- **Expose degraded adopted craft:** compare the integrated result against the target reference and original candidate demo to catch lost typography, clipped lighting, broken spacing cadence, blurred resting transforms or weakened interaction feel.
- **Challenge premature convergence with stronger mature work:** identify what the favored choice actually observed vs. assumed, and bring forward concrete observable visual deltas alongside demonstrably stronger mature references or adoptable implementations.

Ground every critique in visible evidence, actual viewing conditions and the product's goals—never manufacture disagreement or substitute arbitrary stylistic preference. When evidence genuinely supports keeping the current choice, say so clearly. Return the strongest case to retain or revise, specific independent alternatives, unverified assumptions and the next decisive check; the main agent retains the final decision.

## Targeted capture and GUI isolation with ui_experience_observer

Basic browser navigation and screenshot checks remain ordinary work for the main agent, `ui_researcher` and `ui_integrator`. Invoke `ui_experience_observer` only when extended multi-step state control, cross-surface episode reproduction, tight optical inspection or authorized native-GUI observation warrants dedicated attention.

Focus observation on complete **experience episodes and craft-retention deltas**, not isolated static frames:

- **Side-by-side target-demo vs. integrated-UI comparisons** under matched viewport, theme, font, content density and event phase, keeping **Target Reference**, **Candidate Demo**, **Local Trial** and **Integrated UI** identities explicit.
- **Immutable pre/post artifact filenames** (for example, distinct `-trial` and `-post-fix` filenames) so earlier visual evidence is never overwritten on disk and before/after changes remain auditable.
- **Tight optical craft crops** alongside normal-scale views to inspect text rasterization sharpness (catching resting 3D blur), hairline contrast, concentric radii, focus rings and alignment rails.
- **Temporal and ergonomic episode checks:** trigger → first feedback → progression → settlement → reversal/interruption; mid-transition `inert`/focus synchronization during flips, drawers and dialogs; focus return after Stop, Save failure or dialog dismissal; and mobile (`390px`) first-screen occupancy, horizontal table scroll affordances and sticky/toast non-occlusion.

**Coordinate browser and GUI isolation.** Prefer reproducible browser automation for web interfaces; use native computer operation only when the task genuinely requires it and host permissions authorize it. Never allow concurrent actors to compete for one shared desktop or mutable browser session—use isolated browser contexts/profiles whenever supported, preserve real permission boundaries and avoid irreversible external actions during observation tasks. Distinguish captured media from actually viewed pixels, and never claim natural-time smoothness from sparse frame samples or tool-inclusive timing. Report unreachable states honestly and pivot to another authorized evidence route.

## Absorb findings into one Current Design Model

Auxiliary investigations can span L0–L5 without creating a second design model or competing product plan. When results return, the main agent absorbs them selectively into the single [Current Design Model](evidence-map.md#current-model-meaningful-revisions-and-evidence-archive):

1. **Separate resolved facts from open hypotheses:** identify what design question was actually answered, what conditions were observed and what remains unverified. Keep supporting reports and raw captures retrievable in the archive rather than cluttering active decisions.
2. **Directly inspect pivotal visual evidence:** examine the decisive renders, demos or episodes yourself before committing to a source or direction—avoid redoing low-value exploratory clicks while refusing to blindly ratify a summary.
3. **Reconcile conflicts by conditions, not vote counting:** conflicting observations often trace to theme, content density, font loading, viewport scale or source revision; verify the underlying condition directly.
4. **Route findings to the responsible next action:** decide between targeted follow-up research, direct visual comparison, a bounded local trial, supported integration, craft repair or retaining the current choice ([evidence-to-action rules](evidence-actions.md)). Reopen affected source or composition decisions if a trial loses defining craft.
5. **Preserve whole-product inspection and neutral tooling:** after combining bounded contributions, run a main-agent whole-product pass across shared anchors, density, type, focus, scrolling and real behavior. An agent identifier, completed report or passing test never establishes viewed quality or owner acceptance. Never expand read-only structural inspectors into delegation schedulers, research-sufficiency judges or UI-quality certifiers.

## Direct single-agent equivalence

Subagents, role selectors, model choices, browser drivers and OS permissions are **host capabilities**, not requirements of the portable `ui-spec` Skill. When the host does not expose compatible custom roles—or when a task is compact enough that delegation overhead outweighs its benefit—the main agent performs the exact same multi-tier discovery, high-taste self-challenge, bounded deep-reuse integration, optical/temporal capture and Current Design Model synthesis directly under the same eight-reference foundation and W1–W3 boundaries.
