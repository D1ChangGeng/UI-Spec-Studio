# Evidence maturity must change the next action

Apply these rules inside `init`, optional `shape`, `iterate`, `audit` and
`finish`. The [multiscale map](evidence-map.md) supplies the objects and evidence
relations. **The required constraint is the relationship between an important
choice, its actual support and the scope of the next commitment.** A particular
file, JSON schema, tool or additional approval meeting is not required.

## Before a consequential commitment

Before selecting a new shell, expanding a defining component, committing to a
visual direction or declaring acceptance, identify the decision, evidence
actually inspected, important gaps, dependencies and proposed next action.
Use a small entry in the existing decision record. Update it after a meaningful
observation, comparison, trial or repair, rather than filling it retrospectively
only after the interface has been built.

For each open high-impact gap, name the next evidence-producing action, the
object/state/condition to inspect, and the outcome that could confirm or change
the choice. “Needs more research” is incomplete when it does not identify what
to investigate next. Do the supported next action in the current work; a written
plan is not an executed search, comparison or trial.

Do not extend a dependent visual or experience commitment beyond its supporting
evidence. This is a scope boundary, not a whole-project freeze. Continue
independent supported work. A reversible prototype may investigate an unresolved
hypothesis when its boundaries and learning question are explicit; label it a
probe rather than an accepted foundation. Existing approval or delegation can
authorize a provisional direction, but cannot turn missing observations into
verified quality.

## Gap-to-action rules

| Current evidence condition | Next action | What permits further progress |
|---|---|---|
| The experience model or coverage is incomplete | Study full user tasks and challenge the inventory against actual mature-product navigation, tutorials and flows | Relevant discoveries have include/exclude/open decisions; consequential unknowns have routes to evidence |
| Functional documentation exists but decisive visual relationships have not been viewed | Obtain and inspect actual pages, component demonstrations or suitable visual media | The claim has direct, relevant observations under identified conditions; textual facts keep their separate scope |
| Motion is represented only by endpoints, a poster or unviewed recording | Observe the meaningful trigger-to-result interval, adding reversal/interruption/loop boundaries where relevant | The inspected process covers the specific temporal claim; sparse sampling and unobserved playback remain limited |
| A preferred candidate has only near-identical alternatives | Search a materially different mechanism, composition or expressive direction capable of changing the choice | Comparison reveals distinct strengths/tradeoffs, or justified applicability of an existing validated family |
| Target quality and candidate are credible, but current-project fit is unknown | Run a bounded local trial with representative fonts, content, themes, layout and critical states | The candidate's defining relationships survive the relevant adaptation; failed fit can reopen selection |
| Critical qualities, candidate fit and implementation ownership have support | Expand the supported implementation scope and inspect assembled pages and journeys | Actual integration evidence supports the claims being made, not merely package installation |
| Real implementation exposes a quality gap | Locate its scale/cause: experience model, reference, source, adaptation, content, composition or runtime; repair and recheck | Affected claims and dependent conditions are revalidated without silently lowering the target |
| A shared dependency or evidence condition changes | Revisit affected decisions and claims; preserve unaffected evidence and historic approval scope | Recheck or a specific applicability argument establishes which prior results still apply |
| A low-impact change inherits current, matching-condition evidence | Make the narrow change and verify its local consequences | The inherited rule and scope are identified; avoid restarting unrelated broad discovery |

These are evidence/action relationships, not a fixed task count or time quota.
Research may go through many independent sources when a small object has large
design impact. Similar new results support convergence only after challenging
the question and candidate space. Conversely, evidence-supported routine work
should proceed: more searching without a decision-changing question has a cost.

## Access constraints and exceptions

Keep the original design question when access fails. Seek author/original project,
official public materials, other demonstrations, authorized recordings or a
supported local run. Check whether a substitute supports the same claim: source
code can explain mechanics but does not establish rendered quality; edited
promotion can omit waiting, failure or recovery. Never fabricate unseen states.

After feasible exploration, a focused request for authorized user assistance
names the source, action, required page/state/interval and the decision it will
resolve. Batch related capture needs. Keep analysis, comparison and selection
with the agent. Continue independent work or a clearly bounded probe while a
specific dependency is blocked.

An exception needs a real authorized decision, reason, affected scope and any
conditions or expiry. Record it as an exception; it does not certify the missing
quality. Agent convenience, tool failure and elapsed effort are not approval.
Normal privacy, access, resource-license and safety obligations remain in force.

## Carry reuse into visible implementation

For a consequential choice, connect **design reference → candidate resource →
original demonstration → local trial → adopted source/configuration → integrated
result**. One source can occupy several roles, but explain which role supports
which claim. Headless interaction, icons and text rendering can provide valuable
functional foundations without constituting the whole visual implementation.
Identify what carries the visible composition, material, typography and motion.

Use the largest suitable coherent reuse level and retain the mechanisms that
earned selection. A rewrite creates new verification responsibility. Custom work
addresses an evidenced gap, and its actual result is inspected alongside retained
mature material. Judge the real user's main task, populated states, continuity
and expressive completion rather than the number of imported dependencies.

## Reopen only the affected decisions

A new font can invalidate line wrapping without invalidating service persistence;
a request adapter can invalidate pending feedback without discarding an approved
brand mark. Follow explicit dependency and shared-use links to identify likely
review scope. Recheck meaningful parent composition as well as local details.
Keep passing, failed, blocked and unexamined conditions visible instead of
replacing them with a single maturity score.

At handover, show what is supported now, the actual preview, a representative
piece of evidence for important choices, the unresolved dependency and the next
useful action. The user decides consequential preference or commitment questions;
routine research, implementation and error correction remain agent work.

## Worked decision: a companion panel

Suppose the task is to keep a research conversation readable while inspecting
sources. The map links L1 read-answer/open-source/return, L2 conversation and
panel, L3 citation control/composer, and L4 text measure and visual balance.
A help article establishes that a source panel exists; a screenshot establishes
its visible arrangement. Neither establishes input retention or the open/close
transition. The DDU therefore keeps those claims open and chooses an actual
operation or continuous recording before committing the shared shell.

After comparing meaningful panel organizations, trial the selected source with
long answers, expanded composer content and relevant narrow conditions. Inspect
reading width, focus return, stable input, scroll and moving weight through the
episode. A failure in narrow reading width reopens the layout decision; it does
not invalidate the product's user goal. A passed source import check cannot close
that visual gap. If the same family already passed matching conditions and the
request only changes a helper label, inherit that evidence and perform the
focused text/reflow check instead of repeating the entire product study.

## Three separate claims of success

**Content integrity:** the method contains coherent, specific guidance and retains
previously valid knowledge. **Agent behavior:** actual tool traces and outputs
show that discovery, viewing, comparison and gap-directed action happened.
**UI quality:** the real output shows mature perception, meaningful interaction,
expressive completion and reliable operation. A structural checker can detect
some inconsistent declarations but cannot certify the other two claims.

For comparisons, use independently started sessions, frozen skill variants,
matched tasks/tools/models/budgets and auditable outputs. Keep a structure-only
condition separate from the additional action-guidance condition. Examine
supported convergence as well as deeper exploration; do not reward needless
work or fluent reports. A script replaying authored records is an engineering
fixture, not a real agent experiment. Report unrun comparisons as pending and
avoid claiming a measured design improvement from publication or unit tests.
