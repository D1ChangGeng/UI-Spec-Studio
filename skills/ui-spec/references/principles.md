## Understand the strength of each statement

**Invariants — requirements:** readable content, comprehensible hierarchy, valid task paths, accessible controls, visible focus, accurate state, stable shared structure, coherent feedback, truthful claims, inspectable evidence and trusted business boundaries.

**Implementation invariant:** apply [default-deny original UI construction](component-sourcing.md#default-deny-original-ui-construction) across visible design responsibilities. Use the professional principles below to discover, compare, configure, combine and adapt mature work. Original visual construction requires a qualifying, narrowly authorized [W1–W3 exception](component-sourcing.md#closed-original-construction-allowlist); knowing a technique or recognizing a simple shape does not provide that permission.

**Principles — professional judgment:** type scale, density, measure, spacing, optical corrections, scroll ownership, timing and composition. Apply them within the chosen mature foundation, complete approved design or authorized original scope, considering the task, input method and page family.

**Project requirements:** explicit aesthetic goals and accepted direction govern expression, identity and craft alongside task requirements. **Preferences** guide open choices; **defaults** supply starting treatments; **examples** explain contextual decisions. Conventional palettes, grids or navigation forms remain revisable.

Make typography, content, imagery, composition and interaction serve a clear direction. Professional tools can combine precise tasks with photography, sculpture, expressive type and motion. Product category informs operating conditions; judge the chosen expression in actual use.

## Art direction and expressive contrast

Define the intended character, first impression and memorable relationships from audience, content and aesthetic intent. Assign expressive roles across composition, type, assets, surfaces and motion. Study functional experience, cross-domain expression and adoptable implementations together. An artistic brief needs candidates that materially change subject, composition or movement, shown at comparable completion with the same representative task.

**Expressive Contrast** combines understandable tasks with a distinctive visual language. Identify the familiar relationship, the expectation being changed and the theme connecting them. Contrast may join dense information with open space, technical type with organic form, or immediate operation with a staged result. It can reach navigation, inputs and populated work; identity must survive the first action. The theme may express brand, culture or emotion without literally illustrating each function.

Develop independent expression through mature resource selection, source-backed composition and coherent art direction. A request for an expressive product does not authorize recreating its ordinary controls or workspace. Use W1 for explicitly authorized unique identity/art, W2 for an evidenced and authorized unmet product expression, and W3 for its narrowly necessary mature-source extension; preserve the mature responsibilities around that scope.

Coordinate strong focal elements and supporting echoes. Repair concrete failures in contrast, reachability, motion or resource cost while preserving confirmed expression. Reposition, mask, pause during input or change delivery where evidence supports it. A necessary direction change is explicit. [Verification](verification.md) assesses experience validity and expressive completion independently.

## Inspect from whole to detail and back

Use **Human-centered Visual Inspection & Perceptual Design Analysis** to see relationships, explain their effects and choose/adapt implementations. Start with the actual subject in its viewport at normal use scale. A compressed page overview locates sections but cannot establish natural prominence or small-detail quality.

| Scale | Observe and ask |
|---|---|
| Whole impression | Note apparent focus, attention order, density, character and task entry before close analysis. Does the impression fit this product and use? |
| Composition | Compare region proportions, visible masses, grouping, alignment, negative space and reading path. Which relationship creates balance, rhythm or competition? |
| Functional region | Identify its job, content hierarchy, controls and feedback. Inspect internal grouping alongside its position, weight and dependence on neighboring regions. |
| Component / asset detail | Inspect rendered type, contour, boundaries, padding, icon weight, optical position and surface layers at use size; enlarge a relevant crop to explain an edge or alignment. Retain surrounding context. |
| Return to the whole | Revisit the page after local selection or adjustment, then related views and use. Does the refinement preserve task focus, neighboring quality and product character under growth or change? |

Choose lenses by object and decision; investigate their relationships without independent scores or compulsory checks for every element.

| Perceptual lens | Visible relationships to investigate |
|---|---|
| Hierarchy and attention | Salience, contrast, grouping, reading order and relative action weight |
| Space and proportion | Region allocation, density, gaps, alignment, optical center and boundaries |
| Typography | Glyph shape, weight, line rhythm, tracking, wrapping, mixed scripts and container fit |
| Color and value | Hue, lightness, saturation, colored area, foreground/background contrast and gradients |
| Material and surface | Borders, shadow, transparency, blur, texture, reflection, light and overlapping depth |
| Shape and component craft | Silhouette, radii, stroke, icon scale, internal space and neighboring weights |
| Assets and expression | Composition, crop, clarity, spatial material, graphic language and family coordination |
| States and movement | Focus/selection, feedback, trajectory, speed, settling and object continuity |
| System and identity | Related type, density, color, material, icon and interaction choices across views |

A favicon needs silhouette, negative space and recognition at tab size; a three-pane workspace needs allocation, density and reading continuity. Reuse established judgments where conditions match. Compare important candidates through materially different relationships at comparable content, scale and completion; explain which difference changes selection or adaptation. Deepen observation where the target's quality remains uncertain.

## 1. Hierarchy follows meaning

Give the task and its useful information appropriate prominence. Group labels, values, actions and feedback through type, contrast, position and spacing. Preserve legible supporting information and necessary meaning.

Choose type for character, language coverage and reading. Inspect actual scripts, punctuation, numerals, weights, line height and fallback. Use real localized labels and values to establish hierarchy and rhythm.

## 2. Design tokens express a system

Translate the trialed mature foundation into roles for type, space, color, shape, material, icons, asset crops and motion. Inherit tokens, component variables and shared styles from its explicit design system or validated project system. Establish type/line scales, spacing rhythm, nested-radius relationships and optical icon/text weights through those sources and actual renders; a viewed screenshot alone does not authorize inventing replacement values. Preserve the source relationship while making evidenced local adjustments; numeric equality alone does not establish harmony.

One source can carry many related details. A mature surface token can establish an ordinary solid background, border and foreground relationship without a separate search for every declaration. Its origin and actual applicability must be identifiable; a newly invented value does not become mature merely by being named a token.

Preserve purposeful differences through roles: display and reading type, expressive results and direct repeated feedback can share one system. Separate identity, page-family anchors and local composition; explain exceptional treatments and where they apply. Validate real content and related pages when shared rules change.

## 3. Core scale and composition occupancy

Judge control scale and total composition separately. Stable control sizes can coexist with responsive containers, columns, context areas and outer space.

Inspect readable dimensions, density, dominant mass and distance between related elements. Bounded fluid values can help; viewport ratios provide diagnostics rather than aesthetic scores. Preserve effective browser zoom.

Distinguish layout dimensions, transforms, animated projection, preview fitting and screenshot display scale. A card at the edge of a turn appears narrow without shrinking its layout box. Standardize a moment for static comparison, then observe the full transition at its intended scale and pace.

## 4. Whitespace is relational

Separate padding, group/section spacing and outer margins. Tighten related information and distinguish separate tasks. Judge negative space around the whole composition alongside internal gaps.

Give collections meaningful item widths and gaps. Additional container space can become outer margin or intentional columns according to the task; avoid distributing items without a compositional reason. Width follows content and purpose.

## 5. Optical balance

Judge perceived weight from visible shapes, text, imagery, contrast, material, depth and negative space at actual viewing size. Bounding boxes and geometric alignment help construct the result; compare how the resulting masses direct attention.

Compact tasks can start visually centered or slightly above center; another placement needs a reason in the composition. Reading begins at a usable top origin, workspaces follow stable navigation, and expressive layouts can be asymmetric. Respect project preferences, reachable controls and short-viewport scrolling.

Inspect the full frame with the actual background. A geometric center can coexist with unequal visible weight from a bright image, dark block or irregular silhouette. Adjust the contributing relationship and view the result again. Across time, watch whether moving highlights displace the intended focus.

Reserve space for the full visible motion envelope: rotation, expansion, particles, shadows and brightness. Observe closest approaches and meaningful intermediate states at relevant viewports. Deliberate overlap is viable when important text, hierarchy and input targets remain clear throughout.

## 6. Page-family anchors remain stable

Within the chosen system, keep shared headers, navigation and content origins predictable at corresponding viewport/scroll conditions. A redesigned composition can establish new anchors; verify its orientation cues. Let each region handle growth.

Switch through related views to observe retained identity, focus, input and selection. Necessary intrinsic changes need deliberate effects on neighbors. Include late content, loading and errors, then inspect both the transition and settled result.

## 7. Choose layout by task shape

| Shape | Primary considerations |
|---|---|
| Public introduction / marketing | Clear subject, meaningful sequence, distinctive direction, primary action and useful further exploration. |
| Reading / documentation | Comprehension, text measure, headings, navigation, links, reading progress and natural document scrolling. |
| Compact focused task | Appropriate form/subject occupancy, near-balanced vertical space, accessible entry and completion. |
| Multi-column task | Related zones, purposeful width allocation, alignment, priority, stacking and independent growth. |
| Workspace / editor | Stable chrome, task density, selection/focus continuity, deliberate scroll ownership and tool reachability. |
| Collection / detail | Predictable item scale, scanability, filters, pagination/virtualization when justified, content-length resilience. |

These shapes can coexist in one product. Let the surface's task, rather than its industry label, govern behavior.

## 8. Motion supports continuity

Give each transition a purpose: feedback, a revealed relationship, orientation or a state change. Choose timing for distance, frequency and task urgency; repeated actions need prompt acknowledgment and low friction.

Observe the whole episode at normal speed, inspect relevant intermediate detail, then return to normal speed. Include trigger, settling and supported reversal/new input. Judge object identity, readable holds, competing motion, moving light/material and attention destination. Stable anchors can preserve orientation while a layer moves. Review reduced motion and representative devices; [interaction](interaction-and-variants.md) owns behavioral details.

## 9. Scrolling is a design decision

Choose document scrolling for continuous reading and content growth, or bounded regions where workspace orientation benefits. Each region needs clear ownership, reachable content, keyboard use and sensible restoration. Scope horizontal scrolling to content whose relationships need it.

Coordinate scrollbars with themes, platform visibility and high contrast. Preserve discoverability, operability and stable content width. Exercise short heights, zoom, virtual keyboards, growth and overlays; actually scroll to verify hidden content.

## 10. Variants are complete designs

Give themes coherent color and elevation roles; give languages natural copy, typography and formatting. Content extremes, permissions and failures retain the default's design quality. Centralize shared labels and state mechanisms where supported.

Coordinate icon stroke, shape and weight with semantic names and usable targets. Group utilities, identity and navigation by actual meaning and use; order them for the project's mental model and inputs.

## 11. Visual judgment and measurements work together

Connect **visible fact → likely perceptual effect → design relationship/cause to test → current-product fit → resource or adaptation choice → inspected result**. A quiet input boundary may remain clear through surface value, surrounding space and text weight together. Seek a mature implementation carrying that relationship; trial it against the actual background and content. A styling value alone cannot explain the effect.

View before measuring. Use geometry, contrast checks, source and traces to test a causal explanation or required engineering property. Keep observation, inferred cause and measured user response distinct. Compare equivalent content, theme, locale, viewport, browser, font readiness and event phase; disclose intentional differences and capture limits. [Verification](verification.md) owns the evidence method.

## Diagnose a relationship before changing a coordinate

For an oversized surface, inspect control scale, dominant mass, reading distance and surrounding space; widening a region or reducing a gap may preserve readable controls. For a low-feeling composition, compare the full frame, asset weight and shell before moving it. Preserve family anchors when solving local balance. Give growing responses/errors space and inspect their neighbors.

Test a bounded change against the observed relationship. Repair shared causes in tokens, shell or components, then revisit affected details, whole views and sequences. Measurements explain and reproduce a change; the actual render supports the perceptual judgment.

## Visual grammar: choose a means for an intended relationship

Use these methods to select, preserve, adapt and diagnose relationships in the established visual foundation. Product purpose, content and accepted direction guide supported parameters; inspect at use size and across relevant changes. These craft explanations do not independently authorize original construction.

### Typography as information and voice

Assign display, heading, reading, control, annotation and data roles before values. Distinguish them through weight, measure, placement and space as well as size. Choose families for letterforms, language coverage and voice; each family should serve a needed role.

Tune line rhythm to the actual face, script, width and density. Break headings at meaningful phrases; test long text, narrow containers, loading and fallback metrics. An uneven display line and sustained reading need different rhythms. Use tabular numerals for comparison where available, retain units with values, and distinguish data from prose without treating all technical content as code. Reference values need revalidation across languages.

### Color as a finite allocation of attention

Give color a role: identity, emphasis, state, grouping or quantity. Establish legible lightness relationships, then use hue and saturation to clarify meaning. Accent area follows the real focal tasks; emphasis spread across every region loses direction.

Distinguish brand accent from status and reinforce state through words, shape or icons. Check text, controls and adjacent surfaces in supported themes and contrast conditions. Restrained color can express character through type, proportion and imagery; rich color can strengthen a concept. Judge the actual composition and contrast rather than an industry palette label.

### Composition as a relationship between masses

Establish the subject, reading path, alignment and rhythm. Repetition supports recognition; meaningful changes in scale, width or placement distinguish roles. Group related information and separate different tasks.

Compose imagery and text together: crop, focal point, aspect and adjacent space depend on the asset. Preserve important details across variants. Asymmetry needs a counterweight; symmetry still needs hierarchy. Precision can express a dense tool's identity. Inspect the first encounter and complete task/reading sequence.

### Depth and materials as a coherent spatial model

Use borders, fill, elevation, transparency and blur to express grouping, overlap, modality or material. Maintain consistent light/depth logic: a shadow implies elevation, an opaque boundary defines structure, and translucency preserves context only while the underlying information remains understandable.

Tie texture, masks and perspective to the selected subject, identity or atmosphere. Inspect edges, alpha, background dependence, contrast and compositing cost; preserve the visual language and readable task when an effect is unavailable.

### Images and graphics as evidence or expression

Choose content, photographs, illustrations, diagrams or procedural graphics by role. Preserve factual subjects and disclose illustrative substitutes. Judge expressive assets through focal, material, thematic or emotional relationships and continued task access.

For quantities, use encodings suited to comparison, explicit scales/units, meaningful order and visible missingness/uncertainty. A relationship diagram preserves topology and meaning before optimizing spacing. Provide text or tables when needed for access or precision.

### The native surface also carries the design

Inspect focus, selection, caret, links, controls, scrollbars, formatting and default errors. Adapt within platform/accessibility expectations. In GitHub Markdown and similar hosts, use supported headings, links, code, tables and images; the host's mature rendering and responsive behavior carry their actual visual responsibilities. Write the product's content and organize its reading task normally. Added artwork or a custom presentation layer retains its own source or authorized-exception responsibility.

## Define a positive, inspectable craft target

State what the finished design should make understandable and what distinct character it should communicate. Identify a reference or a local probe for each defining quality, keeping its content, viewport, medium and actual observation conditions visible. Compare the organization and principal relationships before ornament; inspect identity-bearing details before making minor aesthetic corrections. A mechanically valid layout may still lack a convincing organizing idea, while an expressive composition may still obscure a user's next action.

A [local probe](component-sourcing.md#separate-probes-from-formal-delivery) can answer a bounded research question. Its runnable appearance does not establish a mature implementation source or permission to use an original visual result in formal delivery.

When making a design more expressive, intensify the established idea through type, subject, composition, material and motion rather than accumulating unrelated effects. When quieting the expression, preserve the relationships that communicate its identity. Assess the intended primary action, useful content, proportional balance, practical readability and coherent character together. Judge a proposed improvement by the actual complete render and task, not by adjective, library count or compliance score.

## Understand craft through coordinated decisions

Expand “refined,” “premium” or “textured” into purpose, precise relationships, coherent language, suitable expression and stability in use. Inspect focal intent, optical baselines/icon weight and material rules as content changes. Equal icon boxes can need optical correction. Expression intensity and design maturity are distinct; judge both against the brief, including populated work and repeated use.

Coordinate a mature foundation and valuable additions through type, density, color, material and motion. Judge hierarchy through the first action, working object and continuation. A behavior primitive's visual layer needs its own foundation. Apply the [sourcing method](component-sourcing.md) to preserve observed design value through trials and adaptation. Evaluate any proposed original replacement under the closed allowlist before implementation, then validate its authorized changed scope and measure combined runtime cost. Verification does not retroactively authorize a rewrite.

## Compose mature resources as one visual and interaction system

Read a mature foundation as linked decisions about hierarchy, measure, alignment, spacing rhythm, roles, states and attention. Preserve the quality-producing relationships when altering the product's content or behavior. A font substitution can change line wraps, widths, alignment and the page's perceived balance; test it in context rather than treating typography as an isolated variable.

When combining resources, assign compatible responsibilities. For example, an accessible state/focus primitive can carry interaction semantics while a separately sourced visual treatment supplies character; an animation library must not silently take ownership of the product's confirmed state. Establish one governing language for density, type, color, depth and movement, then adapt supporting sources to it.

Inspect the assembled page and continuous task. Competing accents, simultaneous animation, inconsistent surfaces, duplicate feedback and incompatible densities can degrade otherwise mature parts. Judge the candidate's original quality separately from the quality retained after adaptation. The selected component, existing project elements and platform-native capabilities all remain candidates when they suit the intended experience.

## Broader screen-based surfaces

Use the medium's native capabilities: selectable explanation, copyable commands, alternatives and narrow reading in a README; intended size/distance for slides; readable holds and playback for timed work. Verify the audience's complete path in the actual host.

## Asset craft and page craft reinforce each other

Judge silhouette, contour, material, color, lettering and motion alone and in placement. Coordinate light, perspective, focal point, negative space and weight; a defining asset can reshape layout. Inspect tab-size marks, wordmark spacing, narrow crops and moving highlights behind text. Preserve character and meaning through [asset engineering](visual-assets.md); pair enlarged detail with use-size and whole-placement views.

## Content and typography are one composition

Give titles, navigation, labels, explanations and feedback distinct roles. Compose actual locale strings with stable vocabulary, natural breaks, readable measures and appropriate emphasis. Adjust wording and layout together while preserving facts. Voice carries character; tone follows the task; punctuation follows language and element role.

Use the [content method](content-design.md) for rendered glyphs, contrast, density, whitespace and optical balance. Compare type weight under equivalent content/font readiness in both themes. Correct visible imbalance through contrast, weight or space while preserving working treatments. Let locale, zoom and text-spacing settings reflow important information naturally.

## Connect perceptual detail to its decision and dependencies

Locate a finding in the [design map](evidence-map.md): a local optical defect may arise from L4 contrast, L3 action hierarchy or L1 task order. Explain the observed relationship and inspect the relevant parent composition before choosing a repair. Keep facts, inferred effects and causes to test distinct. L5 checks identify the actual source, adaptation and viewed result rather than stand in for visual judgment.

A changed font, image crop, material or motion envelope can affect multiple pages and states. Follow shared-use links and re-inspect actual neighbors, relevant themes/locales and temporal conditions. A parent design result does not automatically pass because its component records are filled. Use comparable original demos, current-project trials and assembled views to judge retained quality.


## Use visual craft knowledge to discover better implementations

Translate a quality word into a relationship that can be observed, explained, searched for and tested. These lenses help choose mature work; they do not prescribe a house style or replace looking at actual renders. Use the [source-first method](component-sourcing.md#reuse-visual-craft-at-the-appropriate-scope) to find an adoptable realization. Technique knowledge supports inspection and adaptation; recreating its visible mechanism remains subject to default-deny and the closed allowlist.

### Typography: tension comes from roles, not one font recipe

A compact label beside a large heading can create contrast of scale and rhythm; the same treatment on every label removes that hierarchy. Examine the actual glyphs: x-height, cap height, stroke contrast, width, counters, punctuation, numeral alignment and apparent weight. Equal CSS sizes do not produce equal perceived size across families or scripts. Letter spacing that makes a short Latin display line cohesive can crowd Chinese text or break connected writing. A tightly set display face may be inappropriate for repeated reading or small controls.

Search for mature editorial sections, working-input blocks, type specimens and text-motion components with the actual language and content role in mind. Compare line breaks and reading measure, not just a font name. Trial the intended font and its loading/fallback states with short, ordinary and long content. Preserve a source's type-to-space relationship when substituting a family: adjust measure, weight, leading and adjacent icon geometry together if the render warrants it. Keep comparable numeric values aligned where comparison matters. Font names, negative tracking, weight extremes and fixed display sizes are candidates, not universal prescriptions.

For animated type, distinguish glyph segmentation from semantic reading. Combining marks, emoji sequences and connected scripts can break under naive character splitting; word boundaries are language-dependent. Prefer a mature component with a suitable segmentation and accessibility strategy, retain the readable full text, and inspect real mixed-language strings. A weight animation needs a compatible variable font and an acceptable reflow strategy; an image-like wordmark cannot substitute for readable body content.

### Material: inspect a coherent model of light and depth

A surface reads as material through coordinated foreground/background contrast, edge light, translucency, shadow, texture and neighboring layers. Blur alone does not establish glass; an opaque backdrop can make a blur effect visually absent. A thick bright outline can erase a subtle surface hierarchy, while an extremely faint boundary can disappear after a theme or background change. Shadows that disagree with the asset's lighting can make independently polished pieces look unrelated.

Study actual surface demos and asset compositions under light, dark and changing background conditions. Identify whether the important mechanism is a softly decaying light field, an edge-only highlight, a diffuse elevation shadow, a sharp cast shadow, optical refraction or layered opacity. Search for that mechanism rather than the generic word “premium.” Compare it beside text and controls, not only on a decorative empty card. Retain defining falloff, masks, light direction and depth roles through public configuration where possible.

Grain, vignettes, gradients, glass, tinted neutrals, white or pure black surfaces are optional artistic choices. They are neither quality requirements nor automatic signs of cheapness. Evaluate whether their frequency, contrast and placement serve the current content and identity. Texture can collapse into dirty-looking noise at a small size; moving illumination can temporarily defeat text contrast; multiple material systems can compete. Fix the actual relationship instead of enforcing a palette blacklist. Accessibility targets use their applicable standards and real rendered colors, not a borrowed style recipe's numeric floor.

### Composition: preserve useful asymmetry and spatial relationships

Compare visual mass and attention across the entire usable frame. The geometric center of a box differs from the perceived center of an image, a dense text region or an irregular symbol. A visually dominant asset can counterbalance a small task area without making every component larger. Negative space can separate responsibilities or isolate the main action; oversized gaps can instead sever a needed association. Judge the intended reading order and task, not symmetry alone.

Research complete pages or blocks when their resolved composition is the value. A source's cropped subject, editorial margin, display line and action placement may be one connected system. Preserve those relationships when adapting content; copying only the corner radius or background abandons much of the reusable craft. Inspect extreme content, short heights and narrow views before reproducing a desktop composition at smaller scale. For moving subjects, include the complete motion envelope and brightest frame in the spatial budget.

### Expression stays available in professional work

Product categories explain tasks, information and operational constraints; they do not set an artistic-intensity ceiling. A professional tool may use photography, spatial imagery, distinctive type or expressive feedback throughout populated work. Build an understandable primary action and a readable task flow while developing the chosen identity. A strong empty hero followed by generic working screens does not fulfill that promise.

When expression interferes, locate the specific contrast, timing, density, clearance or performance failure. Adjust placement, crop, local intensity, phase or delivery while retaining the accepted direction where feasible. Do not automatically “repair” all professional products into a restrained dashboard. Conversely, a quiet brief should not inherit a dramatic source demo merely because it was easy to find. Compare intended qualities through actual use and allow new resources to revise the art direction.
