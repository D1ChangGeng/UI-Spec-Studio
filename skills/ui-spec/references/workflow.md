## Operate on the current scope

The main path is `init → iterate → finish`; `audit` runs throughout and optional `shape` resolves consequential uncertainty wherever it appears. Resume or combine operations from current evidence: a local refinement can finish in one round, while an approved product can enter integration directly.

Run two revisable lines of work together inside every operation:

1. **Experience & judgment line:** intended experience → task model & taste bar → mature-product research → actual evidence → professional judgment.
2. **Sourcing & realization line:** wide multi-tier resource discovery → meaningful comparison → local trial & source-preserving adaptation → implementation → real verification.

Either line can reopen the other—a runtime layout or craft failure can revise experience or source choices, while new product evidence can reshape an increment while independent regions proceed. Use [evidence maturity](analysis.md#reason-about-uncertainty) and [evidence-to-action rules](evidence-actions.md) to choose further research, a bounded trial or broader implementation: keep dependent commitments provisional while high-impact evidence is missing, name the specific next evidence-producing action and falsifying observation, and never let one blocked source freeze independent supported work. Apply [default-deny original construction](component-sourcing.md#default-deny-original-ui-construction) across all operations: every consequential visual responsibility needs a mature source, faithful authorized design basis or qualifying authorized W1–W3 scope before formal implementation.

## Make consequential design work observable

Treat the conversation as an accurate window into professional design execution without creating a parallel reporting system. Internal work generates evidence; the Current Design Model maintains active understanding; selective conversation events expose consequential findings, decisions and results while work can still respond to them.

Trigger a **Design-Significant Disclosure Point** when a fact, judgment or result materially affects the experience model, visual direction, source choice, implementation commitment, quality or scope—weighing **design impact**, **user observation value** and **quality realization value** together (no quotas, scores or required message counts). Tool calls, saved files and operation labels are not by themselves design events.

### Six design events

| Event | When it matters | Decision-useful substance to present |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Formal scope and quality target** | A formal SPEC begins or its commitments materially change | Experience objective, owned visual/interaction qualities, necessary relationships, inherited foundation and boundary rationale, preserving the overall product goal. |
| **B — Research finding** | Actual evidence changes understanding of the target or problem | Observed subject and viewing conditions, defining visual or temporal relationship, supported judgment and concrete effect on the design. |
| **C — Design or mature-resource choice** | Art direction, a mature resource or a realization strategy will carry consequential quality | Actual candidate or comparison, meaningful tradeoffs, chosen mature source and why it fits, defining craft to preserve and whether a local trial remains open. |
| **D — First reviewable result** | An important visual object or interaction first becomes meaningfully inspectable | Actual trial or integrated result beside the target relationship, qualities observed, retained craft and material gaps—surfaced before broad dependent work inherits it. |
| **E — Material mismatch and reconsideration** | A target mismatch, adaptation failure, craft loss or scope problem changes the next action | Observed symptom, affected relationships, visible facts vs. causal hypotheses, responsible design level, corrective route and required reinspection. |
| **F — Formal increment delivery** | The increment has an operable/renderable result to hand over | Working entry, meaningful comparison, actual adopted sources, scoped observations, remaining gaps and contribution to the evolving product. |

Events can recur, overlap or be absent in a given task; never invent findings or failures to populate the table. Preserve A and F for formal increments, prioritize D for complex visual/interaction work, and merge related low-impact updates into one concise delivery for small repairs. For research-only tasks, identify the inspected comparison or proposal truthfully without implying a runnable app.

### Present consequences cleanly and separate disclosure, review and approval

- **Make the design object the subject.** Present the useful parts of *observation → professional judgment → decision/action → result and remaining gap* (for instance, showing how a multiline composer keeps action alignment while testing whether the reading scroll anchor survives). Keep exhaustive search logs out of the conversation, and separate visible facts from hypotheses, provisional selections from adopted code, and planned checks from executed results.
- **Show real visual/temporal evidence with explicit artifact identity.** Label shown material naturally as **Target Reference**, **Candidate Demo**, **Local Trial** or **Integrated UI** (reusing captures already produced for design work). Static captures establish typography, layout and material; motion, growth, scrolling and asynchronous states require continuous operation or ordered-frame/video evidence ([verification](verification.md)). An empty shell is not a meaningful first result.
- **Let material gaps drive active correction.** When an early result reveals a craft or task defect, repair the responsible target interpretation, source, adaptation, content, composition or runtime layer under the active SPEC, then reinspect the repaired relationship and affected neighbors. Documenting a defect never resolves it. Small differences outside the agreed quality responsibility do not require unbounded refinement.
- **Keep Disclosure, Review and Approval strictly distinct.** **Disclosure** makes an event visible; **Review** examines it; **Approval** is an explicit authorized decision with identifiable scope. Showing an event neither requests nor establishes approval—continue authorized choices, ordinary sourcing and repairs immediately after presenting it. Request a user decision only for an unresolved high-impact direction, a material change to confirmed commitments, an external action, or a concrete W1–W3 exception lacking existing authorization (and only after completing independent supported work). Reuse existing authorization as context without asking again.

## init — establish a useful working understanding

Read the Current Design Model first when continuing an existing product, recovering archived original evidence only as needed. Read requirements, accepted decisions, code, actual screens, dependencies and run/test instructions. Separate required functional contracts, explicit aesthetic goals and revisable conventions. Establish people, primary working objects, task loop, result location, content authority and operating conditions, distinguishing a live product, representative demo and authored visual output. View the normal-scale whole and retain unobserved conditions, asset rights and host production capabilities.

Expand a named reference into the relevant working experience, structure, visual craft mechanisms and temporal relationships. Separate this user's intent, audience expectations and observed reference-product facts (with version/platform conditions). Without a named reference, locate mature task-level and cross-domain expressive benchmarks proactively. Form revisable experience hypotheses and an initial [reference craft breakdown](analysis.md#formulate-an-inspectable-aesthetic-direction-and-reference-craft-breakdown) before broad component searches, then [plan evidence around the experience](analysis.md#plan-evidence-around-the-experience) and build a revisable L0–L5 coverage map ([evidence map](evidence-map.md)). Identify consequential visual responsibilities not yet supplied by existing functional dependencies.

The initial inventory includes the existing project, accepted screens and decisions, working code and tests, source-owned content, languages, relevant user/operator roles, current devices and network conditions, and rights or production constraints for defining assets. Distinguish what must remain functionally true from what is still a visual candidate. Establish the first meaningful action, durable working object, result location and continuation path before treating a named technical workbench as an interface type. Link significant open assumptions to the evidence that could resolve them.

Preserve the **Overall Product Goal** separately from the first **Formal UI SPEC**. Using [minimum sufficient experience closure](analysis.md#choose-a-bounded-increment), propose a revisable structure of candidate SPECs, key dependencies and a justified first priority—choosing a representative core experience with visual quality worth inheriting for a new product. Present that understanding in the conversation (intended overall outcome, current foundation, proposed increments, first-scope rationale and what findings could reshape it). Init is sufficient when you can explain the task, preserve constraints and execute a justified next action; continue an implementation request directly into `shape` or `iterate` without waiting for approval on routine planning refinements.

## shape — resolve just enough direction to build well

Use optional `shape` for requested exploration or unresolved choices affecting interaction model, page responsibilities, art direction, visual identity or feasibility. Complete open parts of supplied direction; for an open brief, deliver an agent-led recommendation backed by inspectable evidence.

1. **Frame the question.** Connect user intent, facts, task and explicit aesthetic goals to the uncertainty that could alter the choice. Keep confirmed needs, supported conventions and hypotheses distinct.
2. **Map experience and evidence.** Establish affected objects, entries/exits, states, page relationships and recovery; select text, rendered views and continuous behavior evidence for the relevant version and platform.
3. **Decompose craft and explore widely.** Research functional, cross-domain expressive and high-completeness implementation sources together using craft knowledge to drive discovery while composition stays fluid. Apply the [perceptual method](principles.md#inspect-from-whole-to-detail-and-back), follow clues through live operation and revise questions as findings emerge.
4. **Compare materially different directions.** Form competing concepts through content, representation, voice, hierarchy, typography, surface material and motion—comparing equivalent populated tasks at comparable completion rather than superficial color swaps. Preserve explicit artistic requirements.
5. **Establish implementation fit.** Inspect candidates' original demos, states, source and licenses alongside existing project work. Seek the highest appropriate reuse level; a source gap triggers further discovery, supported composition or a qualifying W1–W3 judgment—never ordinary custom construction. Read [integration](integration.md) when real business boundaries shape feasibility.
6. **Trial consequential parts.** Run bounded trials with current fonts, real/fixture content, themes, containers and meaningful states. Compare target reference, candidate demo and local trial at matching scale and temporal phases, adapting or replacing candidates whose fit fails.
7. **Carry the decision forward.** Present focused options only when credible alternatives materially alter product direction; clear intent, approval or delegation supports proceeding directly. Record the selected/provisional direction, evidence, adaptations and open questions in the current model and update affected [decision units](evidence-map.md#design-decision-unit).

For repeated or asynchronous work, examine entry preparation, intent-time requests, immediate feedback and authoritative confirmation—balancing latency, freshness, authority and cost. For maintained content, clarify source ownership, defaults/overrides and operator recovery before broad rollout. Converge when consequential questions and materially different alternatives are resolved; an unmet defining quality remains open.

## iterate — deliver one meaningful increment

**Orient and define the slice.** Reconcile feedback, accepted decisions, current code, incomplete journeys and findings. Continue the active SPEC while fulfilling its established objective or resolving its open quality gaps; begin a new formal SPEC only for a distinct cohesive design objective with its own review boundary (additional searches, trials, repairs and execution passes remain internal work). State the SPEC's owned visual and interaction qualities, coupled relationships, inherited current-product decisions, and separate research, primary implementation and affected-product regression scopes. Apply Keep, Split, Merge or Redraw from [analysis](analysis.md#choose-a-bounded-increment) as evidence develops, explaining consequential scope shifts in the conversation while preserving prior goals and unresolved defects.

**Discover, select and establish the mature foundation.** Resolve material uncertainty through `shape` or targeted research. For every consequential visual responsibility owned by this SPEC, identify the mature page, block, component family, asset or validated system that carries it—seeking the highest appropriate reuse level and inspecting original demos before adoption. Shared source provenance can cover many child elements; do not strip simple backgrounds, spacing, borders or motion out of a coherent mature source merely to hand-build them. Use the [sourcing loop](component-sourcing.md#follow-visual-clues-to-answer-design-questions) and [evidence-to-action rules](evidence-actions.md) to close gaps; keep original construction outside normal choices, applying the [closed W1–W3 allowlist](component-sourcing.md#closed-original-construction-allowlist) only for a demonstrated qualifying need with actual scoped authorization. Advance independent supported work while an unresolved sourcing gap stays explicitly open.

**Trial, harmonize and adapt.** Work inside the existing project. Trial high-impact choices under real fonts, content, themes, extremes and critical states before wider expansion. Preserve defining source mechanisms through public configuration, composition, source-preserving de-templating, canonical token harmonization and CSS cascade isolation (`@layer`) before targeted internal edits. Connect business actions at their owner. Keep the selected item's exact variant, revision, public configuration, dependencies, source, editable assets and delivery derivatives with the implementation. Choose the appropriate static, authored-time or live delivery boundary. Test short, typical and long real or explicitly identified fixture strings, realistic content density, locale differences and meaningful pending/empty/error states. Compose copy, type scale and spacing together. Preserve business-fact authority, source permissions and the distinction between illustrative fixture values and real product claims. A representative integration should resolve the most consequential unknown before broad reuse.

Reconsider the active boundary whenever research or trials expose deeper coupling (for example, a mature block unifying adjacent responsibilities, or an animation coupling focus, data readiness and neighboring layout). Update the active SPEC and candidate plans while keeping prior commitments, change reasons and open issues traceable in the Current Design Model and meaningful revisions.

**Integrate and inspect assembled behavior.** Inspect whole → relationships → details → whole, related surfaces and continuous episodes under relevant viewports, themes and reduced motion. Verify both this SPEC's claimed qualities and related-product integrity without automatically repeating unrelated product research. For performance or operating work, observe input → immediate feedback → usable result → authoritative completion → recovery under named conditions, checking position under content growth and effective content after shared/override edits. In addition to the whole composition, examine cross-page anchors, actual-size assets and crops, shared focus and scrolling, listener or motion-engine conflicts, overlapping layers, cumulative runtime cost, cleanup and quiet/loading/failure states. Verify keyboard, touch and reduced-motion behavior as relevant.

**Run a Taste-Critique → Upgrade → Re-Inspect pass before delivery.** Passing functional, DOM and geometry checks is necessary but not sufficient. Open the rendered integrated UI side-by-side against the **Target Reference** and the adopted component's **Candidate Demo**, inspecting specifically for:

- **Aesthetic caliber and distinctiveness:** does it carry an authentic visual identity, or does it read as generic, template-like or AI-slop ([failure signatures](principles.md#diagnostic-generic-ui-and-ai-slop-failure-signatures))?
- **Typographic tension and optical precision:** did intended display/body/mono fonts actually load with sharp rasterization (no resting 3D transform blur), crisp hierarchy and clean measure?
- **Surface, light and radius depth:** are luminance steps, directional hairlines, ambient/contact shadows, dark-mode neutrality and concentric radii intact rather than flattened?
- **Spacing cadence and multi-source cohesion:** do adopted sources share one canonical token grammar, aligned baseline rhythm and uncluttered chrome without Frankenstein seams?
- **Ergonomic poise across viewports:** on mobile (`390px`), is the primary content or editor immediately operable in the first screen without stacked banner bloat, occluded sticky bars or broken table scrolling?

If the render looks flat, generic, cramped or degraded relative to the candidate demo, **do not deliver it passively**. Route each defect to its responsible upgrade—targeted re-sourcing (`component-sourcing.md`), [source-preserving de-genericization and token harmonization](principles.md#source-preserving-de-genericization-and-visual-harmonization), adopted-component rendering-fidelity repair or visual subtraction—save a new immutable post-fix capture and re-inspect at normal scale and across affected neighbors.

**Present and continue.** Deliver the working entry, consequential choices, actual adopted sources (distinguishing studied, compared, trialed and adopted items), representative visuals/episodes, observed local and related-product checks, remaining gaps, approval scope and next useful work in both the conversation and current project records. Surface key findings and the first reviewable result while they can still inform integration.

### Turn subjective feedback into a useful repair

Treat subjective feedback as a concrete experience symptom to diagnose visually before editing; treat a user-suggested mechanism as a candidate unless explicitly required. Map the complaint to verifiable relationships, run a bounded repair, compare before/after at intended scale and propagate shared fixes where applicable:

| Subjective feedback | Primary visual & behavioral causes to inspect | Responsible repair route |
|---|---|---|
| “Too large” / “heavy” | Control scale, content mass, preview scaling, oversized padding or crowded viewport occupancy | Rebalance scale ladder and content region while preserving shared page anchors (`principles.md`) |
| “Jumps” / “janky” | Shifting shared anchors, scroll ownership conflicts, remounted component identity, layout-triggering animation or late font swap | Stabilize anchors, preserve object identity and inspect the full temporal episode (`verification.md`) |
| “Feels generic / template-like” | Unadapted stock components, uniform card grids, default system/sans pairing, missing focal craft moment or cliché accents | Check [generic-UI signatures](principles.md#diagnostic-generic-ui-and-ai-slop-failure-signatures); upgrade via wider multi-tier [sourcing](component-sourcing.md) or [de-genericization](principles.md#source-preserving-de-genericization-and-visual-harmonization) |
| “Looks flat” / “lifeless” | Collapsed surface luminance ladder, missing subtle hairlines or rim highlights, absent ambient+contact shadow depth, timid type weight contrast | Calibrate luminance steps, edge/shadow model and typographic tension ([six calibrations](principles.md#six-operational-craft-calibrations)) |
| “Dark mode feels muddy or harsh” | Pure `#000`/`#fff` extremes or uncalibrated gray inversions, low-contrast borders, glowing/washed-out accents or clipped shadows | Rebuild tinted neutral dark surface ladder, restrained hairline alpha and semantic contrast (`principles.md`) |
| “Feels cramped / noisy” | Competing focal points, boxes/borders around every group, tight line measure, redundant metadata badges or stacked header chrome | Subtract non-load-bearing borders and chrome, restore relational whitespace and unify action hierarchy |

## audit — inspect an identified scope

Respect review-only versus repair scope. Run a **dual-track audit** so neither functional correctness nor visual excellence hides gaps in the other. During audit and finish, compare current claims with real outputs and scoped tests, not with a mechanical inventory-success flag.

- **Track 1 — Functional, semantic and continuity audit:** reconcile routes, navigation, roles, overlays, runtime states, nine experience dimensions and the evidence inventory against actual execution (capturing newly discovered omissions). Exercise real affordances and verify recurring regression points: keyboard focus return after Stop, Save failure and dialog dismissal (never stranded on `BODY` or a hidden control); mid-transition `inert`/`aria-hidden` synchronization with live flip/drawer geometry; narrow-viewport (`390px`) table horizontal scroll affordances and keyboard scroll access; sticky header and fixed toast non-occlusion over navigation or first reading lines; and truthful pending/empty/error/recovery states.
- **Track 2 — Taste and craft-retention audit:** inspect rendered views at normal scale and targeted optical crops against target references and original candidate demos. Diagnose [AI-slop failure signatures](principles.md#diagnostic-generic-ui-and-ai-slop-failure-signatures), degraded adopted craft (lost fonts, clipped glows, resting 3D text blur, broken spacing cadence), cross-source Frankenstein seams, dark/light theme calibration and mobile first-screen ergonomics.

Connect every viewed frame or episode to its perceptual or task impact, governing principle, testable cause and repair scope; check shared dependants when fixing common layers. Keep actually viewed coverage, captured media and declared inventories distinct, preserving `planned`, `pass`, `fail`, `blocked` and `waived` meanings honestly ([verification](verification.md)).

## finish — complete real connection work

Read [integration](integration.md) and execute **reconcile → contract → sequence → connect → verify → deliver**.

Reconcile accepted experience, art direction, reference/resource decisions and actual services, data, permissions and code. Preserve primary actions, continuation and adopted visual craft—including canonical token harmonization, cascade isolation (`@layer`), rendering-fidelity fixes and authorized source-derived ports—across live data, SSR/hydration, loading, denial and error states. Carry exact source, variant, public configuration, rights, attribution, modification ownership, asset masters/exports and maintenance sources into handover.

Connect authoritative content and localization; verify freshness, duplicate actions, concurrency, cancellation, partial completion and unknown results. During audit and finish, compare current claims with real outputs and scoped tests across both design and product tracks, checking experience validity, expressive completion and engineering reliability at their actual boundaries. Any material change to accepted appearance, task meaning or user commitment returns only the affected scope for review while independent work continues; deployment and real-world actions follow actual authorization.

## Continuity with lightweight records

Keep **Current Design Model → Meaningful Revisions → Evidence Archive** coherent inside existing project records without multiplying parallel reports. Update active choices after consequential discoveries, trials or repairs; explain what changed, why and which objects/conditions are affected in a concise revision, archiving superseded candidates and original captures with their source identities rather than leaving conflicting choices active. Use [the evidence map](evidence-map.md) to share one set of IDs and facts across **Overview**, **Design Detail** and **Execution & Assurance**; routine inherited corrections can stay compact.

Act on the same **Current Product UI** across formal increments through four relationships: **Inherit** supported tasks, visual systems and mechanisms; **Extend** them with a cohesive new experience; **Adapt** existing layout, shared styles or state behavior; or **Revisit** earlier choices when new evidence changes fit. Progress is measured by improvement in the user's real experience—including removing clutter, replacing coarse components or merging fragmented controls—not by growing SPEC or file counts. Keep deferred goals and open quality defects visible across scope changes.

Optional `.ui-spec` records and read-only structural inspectors describe declared IDs, paths and relations; the Agent judges category applicability, evidence adequacy and next actions. Preparation templates establish no product facts, and inspector exit codes or query names never grant stage permission or acceptance.

## Apply the practice to the actual medium

Apply the same intent-grounded method to `README` files, documentation, charts, slides and diagrams through their reading task and publishing host. A `README` prioritizes understanding, fit, installation and first use through native headings, links, code blocks and responsive/themed artwork; verify actual host, theme and narrow renders. Static artifacts verify content, links, assets and publication; live products also verify state, timing and services.

Keep design records and the product interface strictly separated by audience. Internal SPEC IDs, evidence classifications and implementation commentary stay in design records unless the product user's own task requires them. Demonstrate promised depth, material and motion through the rendered interface itself—never through UI labels asserting that quality exists.

## Coordinate auxiliary capacity

When independent discovery, bounded integration, adversarial craft critique or complex GUI capture can strengthen the active SPEC, follow [delegation guidance](delegation.md). Auxiliary roles expand capacity inside these same operations while keeping product goal, formal SPEC scope, pivotal visual judgment, final mature-resource selection, whole-product integration and acceptance with the main agent. Without compatible subagents, execute the exact same workflow directly.
