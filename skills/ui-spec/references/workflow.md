# Workflow: lead the work, preserve the design

## Operate on the current scope

The main path is `init → iterate → finish`; `audit` runs throughout and optional `shape` resolves consequential uncertainty wherever it appears. Resume or combine operations from current evidence. A local refinement can finish in one round; an approved product can enter integration directly.

Connect intended experience → evidence questions → research/observation → design comparison → mature resource adoption → actual-use verification. Findings can return work to any responsible step. Use the [evidence maturity judgment](analysis.md#reason-about-uncertainty) to continue research, run a bounded trial or expand implementation. Effort follows impact, uncertainty and potential improvement within the task's scope. Research depth and routine progress are professional judgments, not added approval stages.

## init — establish a useful working understanding

Read requirements, accepted decisions, code, actual screens, dependencies and run/test instructions. Separate required behavior, explicit aesthetic goals and revisable conventions. Establish people, primary working objects, task loop, result location, content authority and operating conditions. Distinguish a live product, representative demo and authored output. View the normal-scale whole; retain important unobserved conditions, asset rights and production capabilities.

Expand a named reference into the relevant working experience, structure, visual qualities and time relationships. Separate this user's intent, audience expectations and product facts; preserve relevant version/platform/history. Without a named reference, find mature task-level and expressive references. Form revisable hypotheses before broad resource searches, then [plan evidence around the experience](analysis.md#plan-evidence-around-the-experience): important pages, states, transitions and relationships, with appropriate text, visual and behavioral sources.

Record established facts, assumptions and high-impact questions compactly. Preserve functional contracts and assess current presentation against the target. Init is sufficient when the agent can explain the task, preserve constraints and choose a justified next action. Continue an implementation request into shape or iterate; a planning request ends with its requested decision support.

The initial inventory includes the existing project, accepted screens and decisions, working code and tests, source-owned content, languages, relevant user/operator roles, current devices and network conditions, and rights or production constraints for defining assets. Distinguish what must remain functionally true from what is still a visual candidate. Establish the first meaningful action, durable working object, result location and continuation path before treating a named technical workbench as an interface type. Link significant open assumptions to the evidence that could resolve them.

## shape — resolve just enough direction to build well

Use shape for requested exploration or unresolved choices affecting product model, page responsibilities, identity or feasibility. Complete the open parts of supplied direction; an open brief receives an agent-led recommendation with inspectable evidence.

1. **Frame the question.** Connect user intent, facts, task and aesthetic goals to the uncertainty that could change the choice. Keep confirmed needs, supported conventions and hypotheses distinct.
2. **Map experience and evidence.** Establish affected objects, entries/exits, states, page relationships and return/recovery. Select text, actual views and continuous behavior evidence for the relevant version, platform and use.
3. **Explore and revise.** Research functional, expressive and implementation sources together. Follow clues through reading, playback and operation; apply the [perceptual method](principles.md). Let findings change the next question, source or experiment. Explore independent and cross-domain work where it could improve the result.
4. **Compare directions.** Form a concept through content, representation, voice, hierarchy, type, material and motion. Challenge the leading option with materially different advantages. Compare equivalent tasks/content at similar completion, including populated core use. Preserve explicit artistic requirements.
5. **Establish implementation fit.** Inspect candidates' original demos, states, source and terms, including existing work. Prefer the highest suitable reuse level and identify the actual gap requiring custom scope. Consider compatibility and total delivery cost; read integration guidance for real business boundaries.
6. **Trial consequential parts.** Use current fonts, content, themes, containers and meaningful states. Compare the reference, original demo and local result at corresponding scale and event phases. Adapt, replace or revisit the target according to what the trial reveals.
7. **Carry the decision forward.** Present focused options when unresolved alternatives materially change direction. Clear intent, approval or delegated autonomy supports continued work. Retain the selected/provisional direction, evidence, adaptations and open questions beside the result.

For repeated/asynchronous work, examine preparation on entry, requests on intent, immediate feedback and later confirmation. Decide preloading, parallel work or caching from cost, freshness, authority and actual latency. For maintained content, establish source ownership, defaults/overrides and operator selection/recovery before broad implementation.

Choose research, trial or expansion by what the evidence supports. Similar new results permit convergence when important questions and alternatives have been examined; an unmet defining quality remains open. An implementation request proceeds through needed trials, while proposal-only work labels them pending. Reuse matching-condition evidence and reopen decisions when findings warrant it. Keep records proportional to the choice.

## iterate — deliver one meaningful increment

**Orient and choose.** Reconcile feedback, accepted decisions, current code, incomplete journeys and findings. A continuation request is sufficient: choose and state the next useful outcome.

**Define the slice.** Identify affected pages/states, dependencies, intended user outcome and observation conditions. Keep connected tasks and shared families coherent.

**Research and select.** Reuse supported direction or resolve material uncertainty through shape. Pair target qualities with concrete implementation candidates and inspect their actual demos. Let discoveries refine content, assets and layout. Use the [sourcing loop](component-sourcing.md#follow-visual-clues-to-answer-design-questions) to turn unresolved questions into further search, comparison or trial. Seek alternative evidence when access fails and consolidate necessary user assistance after feasible research. Bound custom work to demonstrated gaps.

**Trial and adapt.** Work in the existing project. Trial high-impact choices with real or identified fixture content, fonts, themes, extremes and states before expansion. Preserve valuable source relationships through configuration, composition and focused changes. Connect business actions at their owner. Recheck affected mechanisms and replace candidates whose fit fails.

**Carry the concrete implementation conditions.** Keep the selected item's exact variant, revision, public configuration, dependencies, source, editable assets and delivery derivatives with the implementation. Choose the appropriate static, authored-time or live delivery boundary. Test short, typical and long real or explicitly identified fixture strings, realistic content density, locale differences and meaningful pending/empty/error states. Compose copy, type scale and spacing together. Preserve business-fact authority, source permissions and the distinction between illustrative fixture values and real product claims. A representative integration should resolve the most consequential unknown before broad reuse.

**Integrate and inspect.** Compare whole → region → detail → whole, related views and continuous use under relevant conditions. Check populated core tasks, long content, repeated operation, failure and reduced motion for usability and expression. Locate gaps in experience understanding, reference, resource, adaptation or combination; return to that level and recheck. Make necessary changes to accepted direction explicit.

For performance or operating work, observe the sequence through input, usable result, authoritative completion and recovery. Compare under named conditions. Separate feedback speed, actual task time and resource consumption; inspect position under content growth and effective content after shared/individual updates.

**Inspect the assembled responsibility boundary.** In addition to the whole composition, examine cross-page anchors, actual-size assets and crops, shared focus and scrolling, listener or motion-engine conflicts, overlapping layers, cumulative runtime cost, cleanup and quiet/loading/failure states. Verify keyboard, touch and reduced-motion behavior as relevant. A pass for an isolated component does not establish coherence after multiple resources, real services and content are combined. Repair the responsible source, adapter, composition or state owner, then revisit the affected states and pages.

**Present and continue.** Supply the working entry, important choices and inspected outcome. Connect goals, evidence, actual adoption, retained mechanisms and adaptations. Distinguish studied, compared, trialled and adopted resources; use representative frames/intervals where useful. State consequential gaps, observed conditions, approval scope and next useful work. The user need not restate the method.

### Turn subjective feedback into a useful repair

Treat an observation as an experience to investigate; a suggested implementation is a candidate unless explicitly required. For “too large,” inspect control scale, content mass, preview scaling and whitespace. For “jumps,” inspect shared anchors, scroll, component identity and timing. Test the likely cause with a bounded change, compare the result and propagate shared fixes where applicable.

## audit — inspect an identified scope

Respect review-only versus repair scope. Reconcile routes, navigation, roles, overlays and tasks; inspect structure, families, regions and details, then the complete journey. Use [verification](verification.md) for coverage and evidence.

Connect a viewed frame/interval to perceptual or task impact, the relevant principle, a cause to test and repair scope. Use the nine experience dimensions to find missing responsibilities. Shared issues need related-page/sequence checks. Distinguish actually viewed coverage, captured media and the inventory; preserve pending, blocked, failed and approved-exception meanings.

## finish — complete real connection work

Read integration and follow **reconcile → contract → sequence → connect → verify → deliver**.

Reconcile the accepted experience, art direction, reference/resource choices and actual services, data, permissions and code. Preserve primary actions and continuation while resolving missing behavior at its owner. Connect complete tasks, inspect real content and asynchronous/error states, and verify both visual and product outcomes. Check experience alignment, expressive completion and engineering reliability at their actual boundaries.

Carry resource configuration, attribution, modification ownership, asset masters/exports, delivery variants and rights into handover. Verify controlled state, delayed responses, combined runtime behavior and necessary fallbacks. Connect authoritative content and localization; exercise relevant freshness, duplicate actions, partial completion and unknown results. Identify the observed conditions and responsible maintenance sources.

A material change to accepted appearance, task meaning or user commitment returns the affected decision for review; continue independent work. Deployment and real-world actions follow existing authorization. Static, local and service-backed products finish against their respective content, host and service boundaries.

## Continuity with lightweight records

Keep accepted direction, current outcome, key evidence/choices, shared rules, reviewed conditions and next priority in existing project records. The optional `.ui-spec` manifest records declared coverage, scoped approval and connection evidence; individual scopes retain their own maturity. Keep one current implementation with Git history. Records support continuation; the host owns session persistence and context management.

## Apply the practice to the actual medium

Apply the same method to README, charts, slides and explanations through their reading task and host. A README can prioritize understanding, fit, installation and first use using native headings, links, code and artwork. Study relevant publications; inspect actual host, theme and narrow renders. Static work verifies content, links/assets and publication; live work also verifies state and services.

## Use the evidence map inside each operation

In init, form a revisable scope and task-derived coverage map. In shape, connect candidate decisions to real observed sources and competing directions. In iterate, update affected decision units after discoveries, trials and repairs. In audit, reconcile the inventory with real navigation, states, conditions and dependencies, including newly discovered omissions. In finish, trace accepted qualities through adopted code/assets to actual product-boundary evidence.

Use [the map](evidence-map.md) and existing design records to expose the current Overview, selected Design Detail and Execution & Assurance. Required decision relations do not require a fixed schema or one document per level. A routine inherited correction can remain compact. Treat record completeness, actual execution and user-visible quality as different observations.

## Make the evidence gap select the next work

Before expanding a consequential choice or declaring its completion, read and apply [evidence-actions.md](evidence-actions.md). For every open high-impact question, identify the next actual search, view, comparison, trial or recheck and the result that would change the choice. Keep dependent broad implementation provisional while its critical evidence is missing. Continue supported independent work or a scoped reversible probe; do not turn one blocked source into a whole-project freeze.

After the action, record the new observation and its effect on the decision, not just the intention to investigate. Reopen only the affected scope when a shared font, layout, asset, adapter or observation condition changes. Supported low-impact work should converge and proceed rather than repeat broad discovery.

## Two revisable lines of work and maintained current understanding

Follow experience intent → task model → mature-product research → actual evidence →
professional judgment alongside resource discovery → meaningful comparison →
trial/adaptation → implementation → real verification. These lines inform each
other in every operation. A runtime layout failure can reopen an experience or
source choice; new product research can change the page model while implementation
continues in independent supported regions. No record status makes progress
irreversible.

At init, read the Current Design Model first and recover relevant original evidence
only as needed. During shape and iterate, update active choices after meaningful
findings; explain the change, its reason and affected objects in a concise revision.
During audit and finish, compare current claims with real outputs and scoped tests,
not with a mechanical inventory-success flag. Archive superseded candidates and
past conclusions with their source identities instead of leaving contradictory
choices in the current view. Keep original evidence, material approval decisions
and informative failures traceable.

The structural inspector reports fields, IDs and declared relations; the Agent
judges whether absent categories matter, whether evidence is adequate and what
work follows. Continue low-impact supported changes without filling every category.
Preparation templates establish no product facts. Query names and successful
inspector exit codes are not stage permissions or acceptance decisions.


## Carry mature visual work into the same design increment

In **init**, identify important visual responsibilities not yet supplied by the current functional dependencies. In optional **shape**, use craft knowledge to direct real-reference and high-completeness resource discovery while the composition is still adjustable. In **iterate**, select the useful reuse scope, inspect original demos, trial genuine content and integrate retained mechanisms through configuration/composition before targeted internal changes. In **audit**, compare the original resolved relationships with the complete running task. In **finish**, retain exact source, variant, configuration, rights, adaptation ownership and actual outcome at the product's boundary.

These are uses of the existing workflow, not five new gates. New resource discoveries can revise art direction, and integration failures can reopen selection. An adequately supported routine change can inherit a proven choice. State what the mature source actually supplied; do not finish research with an unexamined return to custom recreation. The [sourcing method](component-sourcing.md#reuse-visual-craft-at-the-appropriate-scope) guides these professional decisions; structural tools do not choose the route.
